# 1  工艺装备申请单通用要求

## 1.1  明确工装用途，说明本套工装用于定位和安装的组件和部件。例如：本工装用于定位安装C919飞机左侧吊挂盒段骨架。

## 1.2  给出工艺装备设计依据（工程数据集、制造数据集、样板、样件、量规等），明确数据集版次和存放位置。

## 1.3  明确协调方案，说明哪些部位采用标准工艺装备协调，哪些部位采用工程数据集协调。例如，机翼上、中、下接头的定位器按标准工艺装备协调，其余零件的定位器按工程数据集协调。

## 1.4  给出所安装零件、组合件、部件的工序状态。

## 1.5  给出工装结构形式，包括工装主体材料、定位方式、工装尺寸和重量要求、最低点距离地面的要求、是否旋转（如是可旋转指出是气动旋转、电动旋转、手动旋转）等；

## 1.6  给出工装与飞机产品之间的定位方式。

## 1.7  给出工装使用设备的名称和型号。

## 1.8  使用单位工艺员应明确工艺装备必须保证的飞机产品关键要素的具体内容和工艺装备必须保证的其它关键要素的具体内容。填写规范：如果有关键要素，应写为“本工装必须保证的关键要素：XX”；没有上述内容视为没有关键要素。

## 1.9  需要更改设计时，用清楚方式（文字描述、截图等方式）描述更改内容。

## 1.10  有特殊需求定检的工装应在申请单中注明。

## 1.11  需要进行功能性分析的应在申请单中注明。

## 1.12  其他要求

### 1.12.1  装配工装质保架份为30架份，零件工艺装备质保期限为3批次；

### 1.12.2  工装所有定位面、活动或可拆件标记出工装图号及其零组件件号；

### 1.12.3  在满足强度要求的条件下，应尽量减轻工装重量；

### 1.12.4  去除工装上的所有锐边。

### 1.12.4  新版申请单技术要求中只包含更改原因和本次更改内容，已在上一版次提过的内容不再赘述。

## 2.1 The function of the standard process equipment, the drawing number and name of the process equipment manufactured and coordinated with the standard process equipment, and whether it is used for inter-factory coordination and exchange.

## 2.2 The structural form and separation surface of standard process equipment shall be attached with schematic diagram if necessary.

## 2.3 Manufacturing, coordination basis and matching relationship of standard process equipment.

## 2.4 Dimensional (fit) tolerance and geometric tolerance of intersection hole and working surface.

## 2.5 Other special requirements, such as process clearance, process datum plane, datum hole, datum line, etc.

3 Technical requirements of assembly process equipment application form

## 3.1 Assembly contents completed on assembly tooling (mold frame, assembly fixture).

## 3.2 Define the assembly process and list the drawing number, name and quantity of installed parts, assemblies and components according to the process sequence.

## 3.3 Structural form, size and fitting accuracy of important locators, clamps and jigs.

## 3.4 Process reference, positioning reference and positioning method of parts and components.

## 3.5 Structural form, size and fitting accuracy of inspection parts.

## 3.6 The method of putting the product on and off the shelf and its installation position, status and working height from the ground in the assembly tooling (including the common working status of the product in the moving and overturning tooling) and the information to be avoided.

## 3.7 Other special requirements on the process, such as process clearance, process reference plane, process margin, etc.

## 3.8 The structural form of assembly process equipment shall be attached with schematic diagram.

4. Technical requirements of the application form for finishing process equipment

## 4.1 Process status of parts, assemblies and components processed by finishing process equipment.

## 4.2 Precision and surface roughness of parts, assemblies and components after machining.

## 4.3 Measuring tools, cutting tools, cutting parameters and machining head stroke used.

## 4.4 Requirements for foundation.

## 4.5 The structural form of the processing head shall be attached with a schematic diagram.

## 4.6 Other special requirements.

5 Technical requirements of sheet metal process equipment application form

## 5.1 Positioning and clamping method of parts on tooling, dimension and tolerance of positioning hole, marking requirements (edge line, margin line, end line, etc.).

## 5.2 For multi-process forming parts, the forming sequence and the forming state, size (or schematic diagram) of the process and the relevant process tooling drawing number shall be stated.

## 5.3 The tooling is used to form more than two parts, and the placement and spacing of the parts shall be specified.

## 5.4 Tooling with special requirements shall be attached with schematic diagram and reference tooling.

## 5.5 For the type of tooling, the specification of the tooling name is the type tire (with pressure) and the type tire. The type tire (with pressure) is used for hydraulic press forming, and the type tire is used for manual forming.

## 5.6 The drawing die shall give the drawing direction, size and quantity of positioning holes.

## 5.7 The structure form of male and female dies shall be described for the inspection die and die.

## 5.8 The drawing and bending die shall give the hole diameter and hole spacing of the equipment used.

## 5.9 If there are special requirements for the selection of tooling materials, the material grade and quenching hardness shall be given.

6 Technical requirements of non-metallic process equipment application form

## 6.1 The shrinkage rate of the product shall be given for the thermoplastic mold.

## 6.2 Specific material code, quenching hardness and structural form (or schematic diagram) shall be specified in case of special requirements.

## 6.3 Number of parts formed on the mold.

7. Contents of technical requirements of mechanical processing process equipment application form

## 7.1 The positioning method, main process datum and clamping method of the parts on the fixture, and the schematic diagram shall be attached.

## 7.2 The shape, placement status, process, size and brief process sequence of the parts processed on the fixture.

## 7.3 Number of parts processed simultaneously on the fixture.

## 7.4 The drawing number or size of the special tool used with the fixture (used to check whether it interferes with the tooling), the guide form, the tool tracking method and the stroke, etc.

## 7.5 Type of machine tool used for tooling, and necessary parameters of machine tool (closing height, T-slot width of workbench, tool type).

## 7.6 Manufacturing basis, processing position, processing method (processing process), process status (process digital model), and symmetry information.

## 7.7 Whether the tool is required to be aligned, whether error prevention is required, and how the tooling is connected to the machine tool.

## 7.8 Other special requirements.

## 8.1 Assembly drawing number of components and part drawing number of components.

## 8.2 Positioning and clamping method of parts on the fixture, size and quantity of positioning holes.

## 8.3 Materials for tooling selection.

## 8.4 If there are special requirements, the correction profile and weld avoidance requirements shall be specified.

## 8.5 Weight, overall dimension and lifting requirements for large tooling.

9 Technical requirements of welding process equipment application form

## 9.1 The assembly drawing number and the number and quantity of the parts involved in the combination welding, and the welding method and welding method shall be described according to the process sequence.

12 Technical requirements of special tool application form

## 12.1 Tools

a) Indicate the materials of processed parts or assemblies;

b) Describe the size and tolerance of holes, grooves and other parts to be processed in language or draw a schematic diagram of parts to be processed to indicate the size and tolerance;

c) Indicate the front and rear guide diameter, length, and tolerance of the fitting position of the front and rear guide tools that are matched with the fixture;

d) Indicate the specific requirements for the connecting parts or working methods of the handle, connecting hole, etc. to be connected with the machine tool, tool, etc;

e) Indicate other special technical requirements related to this tool application.

## 12.2 Measuring tools

a) Describe the datum, size and tolerance of the inspected part in language or clearly express the datum, size and tolerance of the inspected part in graphics;

b) The function of the template class shall be indicated and the figure and tolerance shall be drawn;

c) Note other specific requirements related to the design of the measuring tool.

## 12.3 Tools

a) Indicate the function;

b) Mark the specific requirements of relevant dimensions, tolerances, etc;

c) Indicate whether the space is limited or not;

d) Indicate special surface treatment requirements;

e) Indicate other specific requirements related to the design of the tool.
