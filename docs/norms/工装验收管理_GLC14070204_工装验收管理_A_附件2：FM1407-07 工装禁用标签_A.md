# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装图号。

Inspector of the quality engineering room of the tooling user fills in the tooling drawing number.

（2）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装套数号。

Inspector of the quality engineering room of the tooling user fills in the number of tooling sets.

（3）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装禁用的原因，在相应的选项框内打“√”；如选择“其它原因”，在该位置填写具体的原因。

Inspector of the quality engineering room of the tooling user fills in the reason for disabling the tooling and tick "√" in the corresponding option box; If "Other reasons" is selected, fill in the specific reasons in this position.

（4）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员盖章并注明日期。

Inspector of the quality engineering room of the tooling user shall stamp and indicate the date.
