# 5  规定

## 5.1  铺叠工装使用清洁

### 5.1.1  使用部门在使用铺叠工装进行铺叠工序前需要对工装进行清理，保证工装进入净化间前工装表面及工装框架无异物。工装的靶点孔，定位孔等地方不能有树脂残留。清除工装表面和抓紧条上的树脂和胶膜残留。金属网和真空槽在进入净化间之前清洁干净。

> **English:** Common Used Tooling for sheet metal, machining, welding, nonmetal and component assembling, such as rapping-check dies, trimming-check dies(fixtures), accessory check fixtures, and welding check fixtures, as well as the assembly tooling commonly used on production and inspection shall be calibrated prior to being as inspection (tooling of part fabrication is calibrated before using for each batch; component assembly tooling is calibrated before using for each ship-set).

### 5.1.2  检查工装表面质量，如果存在脱模剂或树脂堆积要进行打磨处理。铺叠工装面的清洁要手工打磨，用羊毛垫或百洁布打磨工装表面，用干净的抹布将污染物去除，最后用丙酮或酒精清洁工装。

> **English:** Check the surface quality of tooling. If there is release agent or resin accumulation, polish it. The cleaning of the layup tooling surface shall be done by hand. The surface of the tooling shall be polished with wool pad or scotch brite. The pollutants shall be removed with clean cloth. Finally, the tooling shall be cleaned with acetone or alcohol.

### 5.1.3  用百洁布机械打磨工装表面，以画圈的方式打磨来帮助聚集的树脂和脱模剂分散。

> **English:** Mechanically sanding the tooling surface with Scotch Brite in a way of circling to scatter the gathered resin and release agent.

### 5.1.4  用丙酮或酒精清洁工装，用溶剂将抹布润湿，从工装的一边向另一边清洁，整个工装表面都要求清洁，并且及时更换被污染的抹布。

> **English:** Clean the tooling with acetone or alcohol, wet the cloth with solvent, clean from one side of the tooling to the other. The whole surface of the tooling shall be cleaned and the contaminated rag shall be replaced in time.

### 5.1.5  打磨完成工装后，工装准备人员按工艺要求涂抹脱模剂。

> **English:** After finishing the grinding of tooling, inform the tooling preparation personnel to apply the release agent according to the process requirements.

> **English:** 5.2

> **English:** Before the layup tooling enters the clean room, it is necessary to visually check the thermocouple socket to ensure that there is no falling off or damage, check whether the thermocouple wire connected on the tooling is damaged, and check whether the thermocouple wire is fixed falling off. If the above situation is found, the tooling preparation personnel shall fill in FM 150-49 and send the tooling for repair. If the thermocouple wire is to be re installed, it is necessary to install the thermocouple wire to the position required by the heat distribution report.

## 5.2  铺叠工装进入净化间前需要目视检查热电偶插座，确保无脱落、无破损，检查工装上连接的热电偶线是否破损，检查热电偶线是否有固定脱落情况。如发现上述情况，使用单位填写FM

### 5.3.1  每季度定期检查铣切工装上的密封条，将磨损的密封条更换。

> **English:** Checks sealing strip on milling tooling quarterly and changes worn ones.

### 5.3.2  每季度定期检查铺叠、铣切工装真空接头，如有泄漏损坏用FM 1

> **English:** Checks vacuum cup quarterly, if there is any leakage or damage, use form FM 150-49 to apply for repair or replacement to Technical Office.

### 5.3.3  每季度检查铣切工装表面和铣切边缘，如果发现任何表面或边的损伤要记录下来并汇报给工艺员与工艺装备室，工艺员与工艺装备室工装主管人员协商处理。

> **English:** Check surface ad milling edge of milling tooling quarterly, if there is any damage on surface of edge, make record and report it to planner and Tooling Office, the planner will deal with tooling supervisor of Tooling Office.

### 5.3.4  每季度检查铺叠工装上的抓紧条，如果开胶或开焊区域大于大约2英寸要用FM 150-49提请工艺装备室返修或更换铺叠工装上的抓紧条。

> **English:** Check grip strip on layup mandrel quarterly, if tackless area is bigger than 2 inches, use form FM 150-49 to apply for repair or replacement to Tooling Office.

### 5.3.5  每季度检查铺叠工装表面，如果发现表面损伤或者树脂堆积，要记录下来并汇报给工艺员与工艺装备室，工艺员与工艺装备室工装主管人员协商处理。

> **English:** Check tooling surface of layup mandrel, if there is any damage or resin accumulation, make record and report it to planner and Tooling Office, the planner will deal with tooling director of Planning equipment department .

### 5.3.6  每季度定期检查工装上的工装标识牌，固定已松动的标识牌。每季度定期检查工装热电偶线及其绝缘层，固定已松动的热电偶盒和热电偶线的绝缘层。

> **English:** Check tooling nameplate quarterly, fix loose ones. Check tooling’s thermal couple wire and its insulating layer quarterly, fix loose thermal couple box and insulating layer of thermal couple wire.

## 5.4  工装存放与运输

### 5.4.1  工装存放时不能直接接触地面，存放环境干燥、无腐蚀性气体。

> **English:** Tooling shall not be stored on the ground directly, the storage environment shall be dry and with no corrosive gas.

### 5.4.2  工装长期存放时需要对工装表面进行保护，工装表面保护禁止采用涂油方式。

> **English:** It is necessary to protect the surface of tooling when it is stored for a long time. Oiling is prohibited for the surface protection of tooling.
