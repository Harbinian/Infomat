# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）-（12）为新制工装的合格证明。

(1) - (12) is Certificate of conformity of new tooling.

（1）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set) or the second set XX tooling".

（2）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装图号。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling drawing number.

（3）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装版次。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling revision.

（4）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装系列号。如无工装系列号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in serial number. If there is no tooling serial number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（5）：工装供应商检验人员填写使用工装制造/检验产品的图号。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the drawing number of products manufactured/ inspected with tooling.

（6）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装制造单位名称。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the name of the tooling manufacturer.

（7）：工装制造单位的检验人员盖章并注明日期，表示工装由工装制造单位的检验人员检验合格。

The inspector of the tooling manufacturer shall stamp and label the date to indicate the tooling is accepted by inspector of tooling manufacturer.

（8）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装数量。如果工装的所有组件需相互配合使用，此栏填写1。如果工装由若干独立使用的组件构成，此栏填写这些组件的数量。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling quantity. If all components of the tooling need to be used together, fill in 1 in this column. If the tooling consists of several independently used components, fill in the number of these components in this column.

（9）：工装供应商检验人员填写新制工装的派工号。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the working number of the new tooling.

（10）：工装供应商检验人员填写工装使用单位名称。

Inspector of the tooling supplier fills in the name of the unit using the tooling.

（11）：工装使用单位/工艺装备室的工装验收人员盖章并注明日期，表示工装由工装使用单位/工艺装备室的检验人员验收合格。

The tooling acceptance personnel of the tooling user/Tooling Office shall stamp and label the date to indicate the tooling is accepted by inspector of tooling user/Tooling Office.

（12）：工装使用单位的产品检验人员填写完成工装验证的产品批/架次。

The production inspector of the tooling user fills in production batch/ ship set of completed tooling verification.

（13）：工装使用单位的产品检验人员盖章并注明日期，表示工装由工装使用单位的验证人员验证合格。

The production inspector of the tooling user shall stamp and label the date to indicate the tooling is verified by verifier of the tooling user.

（14）：工装制造单位的检验人员填写合格证的页次信息。新制工装的合格证为“第1页，共1页”。

Inspector of the tooling supplier in the page information of the certificate. The certificate of new tooling is "Page 1 of 1".

合格证反面填满或该表格升版且工装发生返修时，工装使用单位的工装检验人员应续附新的合格证。新合格证正面填写“工装名称”、“工装图号”、“版次”、“产品图号”，续编页次，即“第 页，共 页”。其中“共 页”的更改采用杠改形式，并在其上方写新的数字，旁边盖检验印章并注明盖章日期。

When the reverse side of the certificate is filled or the form is upgraded and the tooling is repaired, a new certificate shall be attached by tooling inspector of the tooling user. The front of the new certificate shall be filled with "tooling name", "tooling drawing number", "version", and "product drawing number", and the number of pages shall be continued, that is, "page of". The change of "total pages" shall be in the form of bar change, and new numbers shall be written on the top of it, with the inspection seal affixed beside it and the date of the seal indicated.

（15）-（20）为工装返修/定查/搬迁/Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性检查的合格证明。其中：

(15) - (20) is the qualification certificate of tooling repair/periodical check/relocation/damage, wear and integrity inspection of Class III tooling.

a) 工装返修、搬迁时，由负责工装返修、搬迁的工装供应商检验人员填写。

b) 工装定查时，由工装使用单位的工装检验人员填写。

c) Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性检查时，由工装使用单位相应的检查人员填写。

a) When tooling is repaired or relocated, this form shall be filled in by the inspector of the tooling supplier who is responsible for repair or relocation of tooling.

b) This form shall be filled in by the tooling inspector of the tooling user during the tooling periodic check.

c) This form shall be filled in by the corresponding inspector when the tooling user checks the damage, wear and integrity of Class III tooling.

（15）：填写工装返修/定查/搬迁/Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性的检查的完成日期。

Fill in the completion date of tooling repair/regular inspection/ relocation/damage, wear and integrity inspection of Class III tooling.

（16）：对于工装返修/搬迁，填写相应派工号；对于工装定查/Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性的检查，填写“N/A”或“/”。

For tooling repair/ relocation, fill in the corresponding dispatch number; Fill in "N/A" or "/" for regular inspection of tooling/inspection of damage, wear and integrity of Class III tooling.

（17）：填写工装版次。

Fill in the tooling revision.

（18）：对于工装返修/定查/搬迁，填写“返修/定查/搬迁合格”；对于Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性检查，填写“工装完好”。

For tooling repair/regular inspection/relocation, fill in "qualified repair/regular inspection/relocation"; For damage, wear and integrity inspection of Class III tooling, fill in "tooling in good condition".

（19）：对于工装返修/搬迁，负责工装返修/搬迁的供应商检验人员盖章并注明日期；对于工装定查，工装使用单位的工装检验人员盖章并注明日期；对于Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性检查，工装使用单位相应的检查人员签字/盖章并注明日期。

For tooling repair/ relocation, the supplier inspector responsible for tooling repair shall stamp and indicate the date; For tooling periodic inspection, the tooling inspector of the tooling user shall stamp and indicate the date; For the damage, wear and integrity inspection of Class III tooling, the corresponding inspector of the tooling user shall sign/stamp and indicate the date.

（20）：对于工装返修/搬迁，工装使用单位/工艺装备室的工装验收人员盖章并注明日期；对于工装定查/Ⅲ类工装的损坏、磨损、完整性检查，此栏不填。

For tooling repair/ relocation, the tooling acceptance personnel of the tooling user/Tooling Office shall stamp and indicate the date; For regular inspection of tooling/inspection of damage, wear and integrity of Class III tooling, this column is not filled.
