# 工装定检管理程序

# 1  范围

本管理程序规定了样板以外的生产所用工艺装备（以下简称工装）的定期检查（包括定检和定查）要求。

本管理程序适用于民机生产。

本管理程序依据GLG1407的要求制定。

# 5  机构与职责

## 5.1  科技创新部

科技创新部为工装定检的归口管理部门，主要职责如下：

a）工装设计室工装设计员负责编发工装定检技术要求；

b）工艺装备室技术员负责编发定检、定查目录及计划，负责组织使用标准工装/三坐标测量机/力学等测试设备实施工装定检,监督工装定检、定查的完成情况。

## 5.2  工装使用单位

工装使用单位为工装定检的配合部门，主要职责如下：

a）检验人员负责使用激光跟踪仪/常规量具实施工装定检和工装定查；

b）对于使用标准工装/三坐标测量机/力学等测试设备实施的工装定

检，计划员负责工装定检的申请。

## 6.3  工装定检管理要求

### 6.3.1  工装定检内容包括：

#### 6.3.1.1  试验设备和吊运工装按照工装图纸和规范中的参数进行试验。

#### 6.3.1.2 Ⅰ类、装配、检验工装按FM 1407-33中规定的内容进行测量（B777项目的Ⅱ类装配/检验工装按工装定检计划中规定的内容进行测量，具体要求见GLB14070305-03）。

### 6.3.2  定检工装包括：

#### 6.3.2.1 Ⅰ、Ⅱ类工装；

#### 6.3.2.2  具有检验功能的工装；

#### 6.3.2.3  试验设备。

### 6.3.3  新制、搬迁工装的定检要求

#### 6.3.3.1  如Ⅱ类装配/检验工装实施了搬迁，搬迁的工装在投产前应进行一次工装定检。对于A320项目，在工装搬迁之前需要进行一次工装定检。对于与地面连接的非Ⅱ类工装的搬迁，在工装投产前按GLC14

070204对工装的工具球点和工具球面进行验收。

#### 6.3.3.2  对于新制的A220和A320项目Ⅱ类装配/检验工装，在使用工装生产首个产品后需进行一次工装定检。

#### 6.3.3.3  新制或搬迁的Ⅱ类装配/检验工装按产品架份完成工装定检后，工艺装备室发出FM 1407-24规定后续工装定检的实施计划。

### 6.3.4  应根据工装的重要程度、使用状况以及返修频率确定工装定检/定查周期。

### 6.3.4 1 A220项目Ⅱ类装配/检验工装的定检周期不能超过18个月，A320机翼前缘项目Ⅰ类工装的定检周期不能超过3年。如无顾客要求，生产/检验类工装的定检周期不能超过2年，吊运工装定检周期不能超过1年，试验设备的定检周期不能超过3年。

#### 6.3.4.2  对于定检周期需由客户批准的工装，工艺装备室使用FM1407-52向客户申请工装定检周期的初始确定和后续变更。

### 6.3.5  完成FM1407-24中规定的所有工装定检/定查后，工艺装备室应更新下一年度的FM1407-24。此外如发生定检/定查周期变更、添加或删除定检/定查工装，则需在变更工装的申请定检/计划实施定检（使用激光跟踪仪或常规量具）/定查前更新FM1407-24，确保工装定检/定查顺利实施。

### 6.3.6  使用表FM2310-39、FM2310-35编发定检/定查工装目录。通常情况下,工装按GLC14070304进行检查，无需实施定查。工装定检/定查工装的种类及周期等特殊要求需参见本程序适用的管理标准。

#### 6.3.6.1  目录应包括但不限于以下信息：工装图号、工装名称、定检/定查周期、工装使用单位。如有效率工装，应填写相应的套数号。这些信息应与FM1407-24中的信息协调一致。如发生定检/定查周期变更、添加或删除定检/定查工装，则需在6个月内更新目录。

#### 6.3.6.2  定检/定查工装目录的编写按GLB231007-01执行。

#### 6.3.6.3  当客户有要求时，定检/定查工装目录应提交客户审查。

### 6.3.7  工装定检时使用单位不能使用工装。如因工装使用、返修或不可抗拒的原因必须推迟某个工装的定检时，工装使用单位应该在原有效期内填写FM1407-15，说明推迟定检的原因和推迟执行定检的日期，并填写工装无直接影响产品质量的信息说明。推迟定检的日期不能超过原周期的10%或推迟到当前产品下架后定检。对于每架产品下架后实施定检的工装，定检只可向后推迟一架次。在推迟定检期间，工装使用单位办理工装推迟定检的人员将填写完成的FM1407-15的复印件置于推迟定检的工装上，如工装无法粘贴或悬挂FM1407-15，将FM1407-15的复印件置于工装所在工位的明显处。在工装具备定检条件时，应立即实施定检。工装的下次定检日期不受此次推迟定检的影响。

### 6.3.8  对于到达定检周期但暂时不使用的工装，工装使用单位使用FM1407-15申请暂停定检。

### 6.3.9  已办理封存手续的工装不实施定检/定查。

### 6.3.10  对于需要使用标准工装/三坐标测量机实施工装定检的情况，工装使用单位需要使用FM1407-18向工艺装备室申请工装定检。

#### 6.3.10.1  对于到达定检周期的工装，工装使用单位在FM1407-24或FM1407-15规定期限的10个工作日前申请实施工装定检，申请实施工装定检的日期不能超过FM1407-24或FM1407-15中规定的期限。

#### 6.3.10.2  对于暂停定检的工装，工装使用单位在使用工装的10个工作日前填写FM1407-18向工艺装备室申请实施工装定检。申请实施工装定检的日期不能超过重新使用工装的日期。

#### 6.3.10.3  对于需要按架份实施工装定检的新制或搬迁的Ⅱ类装配/检验工装，工装使用单位在产品开工前向工艺装备室申请在产品下架后实施工装定检。

### 6.3.11  实施工装定检时，应先将所有的工装定检内容进行检验、测量。不合格的特性按照GLC14070303进行处置。

### 6.3.12  工装使用单位按FM 1407-24中规定的月份实施工装定查，定

查不合格的工装按GLC14070303进行处置。定查合格后，工装使用单位在工装合格证明的检修记录中填写定查记录并盖检验印章。工装定查的内容需参见本程序适用的管理标准。

### 6.3.13  对于应实施工装定检/定查但仍在使用的工装，工艺装备室发FM 1407-19召回工装。

### 6.3.14  如在80公里半径以内发生里氏5.0或以上地震，对于固定于地面的工装需立即检查其框架是否发生变化，如抽检的20%工装无需返修，则可不检查其余工装。

#### 6.3.14.1  对于震后返修的定检工装需自当前架次起连续进行3架份的工装定检，非定检工装连续进行3架份的全部工具球/面测量，如均合格，可证明工装稳定，定检工装恢复原来的定检周期。否则应继续按架份进行定检/测量，直至连续3架份工装合格，证明工装稳定。

#### 6.3.14.2  如发生其它自然的或人为的灾难，按章节6.14.1对受影响范围内的返修工装进行连续3架份定检或全部工具球/面测量，直至证明工装稳定，定检工装可恢复原来的定检周期。

### 6.14.3  如存在相关客户文件，按客户文件规定执行。

### 6.3.15  工装定检的验收按GLC14070204执行。

# 工装定检管理程序

Management Procedure for Tooling Periodic Inspection

# Scope

This management procedure specifies the requirements for the scheduled periodic inspection (hereinafter called Periodic Inspection and Periodic Check) of tooling equipment（hereinafter called tooling for short）except for template.

This management procedure is applicable to commercial aircraft production.

This management procedure is developed per GLG1407.

# Regulations

### 6.1.1  The test equipment and lifting tooling shall be tested according to the parameters in the tooling drawing.

### 6.1.2  Class I, assembly, inspection tooling shall be measured according to the contents specified in FM 1407-33 (the class II assembly / inspection tooling of B777 items shall be measured according to the contents specified in the tooling periodic inspection plan. See GLB14070305-03 for specific requirements).

### 6.2.1  Class I and II tooling.

### 6.2.2  Tooling used for Inspection;

### 6.2.3  Test equipment.

## 6.4  The periodic inspection/check cycle of tooling shall be determined according to important degree, usage status and repair frequency of tooling.

### 6.4.1  The periodic inspection cycle of type II assembly / inspection tooling of A220 program shall not exceed 18 months. The periodic inspection cycle of A320 Wing Leading Edge type I shall not exceed 3 years. If there is no customer requirement, the periodic inspection period of production / inspection tooling shall not exceed 2 years. The periodic inspection period of lifting tooling shall not exceed 1 year, and the periodic inspection period of test equipment shall not exceed 3 years.

### 6.4.2  For the tooling that need to be approved by customer for periodical inspection cycle, Tooling Office uses FM 1407-52 to apply to customer for initial determination and subsequent change of tooling periodical inspection cycle.

## 6.5  After the completion of all tooling period inspection/ check specified in FM1407-24, Tooling Office shall update the FM1407-24 of the next year. In addition, FM1407-24 should also be updated before periodic inspection application/ planned periodic inspection(using laser tracker or conventional measuring tool)/ check in case of periodic inspection/ check cycle change, adding or reducing period inspection/ check to ensure performing tooling periodic inspection/ check.

## 6.6  Use FM 2310-39 and FM2310-35 to compile and issue the period inspection / period check tooling catalogue. Usually, tooling check is performed per GLC14070304, tooling periodical check is not necessary to be performed. See applicable management instruction of this procedure for special periodical tooling inspection/ check requirements of tooling category and cycle, etc.

### 6.6.1  The catalog shall include but not limited to the following information: tooling drawing number, tooling name, cycle of periodic inspection/ check, and tooling user. If there are duplicate tooling, the corresponding set number shall be filled in. These information shall be in accordance with FM 1407-24. The catalog should also be updated within 6 months in case of periodic inspection/check cycle change, adding or reducing period inspection/check.

### 6.6.2  Period inspection / period check tooling catalogue shall be developed according to GLB231007-01.

### 6.6.3  When the customer has requirements, the periodic inspection / periodic check tooling Catalog shall be submitted to the customer for review.

## 6.7  The user can not use the tooling during the periodic inspection. If the periodic inspection of a certain tooling must be postponed due to the use and repair of tooling, the tooling user shall fill in FM1407-15 within the original validity period to explain the reason for the delay of periodic inspection and the date of postponing the period inspection, and fill in the information that the tooling has no direct impact on the product quality. If the period inspection date of tooling is delayed due to the use of tooling, the postponed periodic inspection date shall not exceed 10% of the original cycle or be postponed to the periodic inspection after the current product is off the shelf. For the tooling that are inspected periodically for each ship set, the tooling can only be postponed for one ship set. For the duration of tooling postponed periodical inspection, the person who deal with tooling postponed periodical inspection of tooling user place copy of FM1407-15 on the tooling of postponed periodical inspection. If FM1407-15 cannot be attached or hung on the tooling, the copy of FM1407-15 shall be attached on the obvious place. When tooling have the chance to be inspected, the periodical tooling inspection should be performed immediately. The next periodic inspection date of tooling will not be affected by this delay.

## 6.8 For the tooling that reaches the periodic inspection cycle but is not used temporarily, the tooling user applies for suspension of periodic inspection using FM1407-15.

## 6.9 Periodic inspection / periodic check shall not be carried out for tooling which has been sealed up.

## 6.10  For periodical tooling inspection that need master tooling /CMM, Tooling User applies to Tooling Office for performing periodical tooling inspection by using FM 1407-18.

### 6.10.1 For the tooling that reaches the periodic inspection cycle, the tooling user shall apply for the tooling periodic inspection 10 working days before the deadline specified in FM1407-24 or FM1407-15, and the application date for implementing tooling period inspection shall not exceed the period specified in FM1407-24 or FM1407-15.

### 6.10.2  For the tooling which is suspended from periodic inspection, the tooling user shall fill in FM1407-18 10 working days before using the tooling and apply to Tooling Office for tooling periodic inspection. The date of applying for periodic inspection of tooling shall not exceed the date of re using tooling.

### 6.10.3  For the class II assembly / inspection tooling that needs to be manufactured or relocated according to the number of units, the user of the tooling shall apply to Tooling Office before the start of the product and carry out the periodic inspection after the product is removed from the shelf.

## 6.11  When performing tooling periodical inspection, all contents of tooling periodical inspection should be inspected and measured. Nonconformance features are to be disposed per GLC14070303.

## 6.12  The tooling user shall carry out periodic tooling inspection according to the month specified in FM 1407-24, and the unqualified tooling shall be disposed according to GLC14070303. After the periodic inspection is qualified, the tooling user shall fill in the inspection record of the tooling certificate and stamp the inspection seal. See applicable management instruction of this procedure for the content of tooling periodical check.

## 6.13 For the tooling that should be inspected / checked but still in use, Tooling Office issues FM 1407-19 to recall the tooling.

## 6.14 If an earthquake with Richter 5.0 or above occurs within the radius of 80 km, it is necessary to immediately check whether the frame of the tooling fixed on the ground has changed. If 20% of the tooling inspected does not need to be repaired, the rest of the tooling may not be inspected.

### 6.14.1 For the periodic inspection tooling repaired after the earthquake, 3 units of tooling shall be inspected continuously from the current sorties, and 3 units of all tool ball / surface measurement shall be conducted continuously for the non periodic inspection tooling. If all periodic inspection/ measurement are accepted, tooling is proved to be stable, the periodic inspection tooling can resume the original periodic inspection cycle. Or periodic inspection/ measurement should be proceeded, until 3 continuous units of periodic inspection/ measurement are accepted to prove tooling is stable.

### 6.14.2  In case of other natural or man-made disasters, 3 continuous units of periodic tooling inspection or all tool ball / surface measurement of affected rework tooling should be conducted per section 6.14.1, until tooling is proven stable. The periodic inspection tooling can resume the original periodic inspection cycle.

## 6.15  Acceptance of tooling periodical tooling inspection is performed according to GLC14070204.
