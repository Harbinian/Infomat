## 5.1  工装评审与审批

### 5.1.1  评审

#### 5.1.1.1内部评审

内部评审按GLC14070202执行。

> **English:** Internal Review as per GLC14070202.

#### 5.1.1.2 厂际评审

中国商飞控制的工装项目需进行厂际评审：

a）评审由中国商飞组织，工装设计室承办，中国商飞和相关部件制造商及有关专家、设计人员参加。

b）完成工装方案设计后提交评审前，工装设计室应事先将评审/审批申请报告（附录A）及评审资料通过数据管理平台或光盘等媒介提交中国商飞评审，评审资料至少应包括：工装设计技术条件等设计输入文件、工装设计相关执行文件标准、工装图样/数据/报告等设计输出文件。

c）评审结果需以书面形式记录并备案。

> **English:** The tooling controlled by COMAC needs inter-plant review:

> **English:** The review is organized by COMAC and undertaken by the tooling design department. COMAC, corresponding components manufacturers and related experts, designers participate in the review.

> **English:** Before the review, tooling design department should send the review/endorse application report (Appendix A) and review material to COMAC through data platform or by disc after finishing the tooling scheme design. The material to be reviewed should include at least: design input files such as tooling order, related standards for tooling design, and design output files such as tooling drawing/data/reports, etc.

> **English:** The review results should be recorded in written form and filed

#### 5.1.1.3 工装评审内容

##### 5.1.1.3.1  初步设计评审(PDR)

初步设计评审是对初步设计阶段成果的评审，完成三维初步设计，目的是确定已具有了正确的设计方案选择，并且能够满足所有的工艺和功能需求。

> **English:** PDR is the review of the results of the preliminary design stage and the completion of three-dimensional preliminary design. The purpose is to determine that the correct design scheme has been selected and can meet all process and functional requirements.

###### 5.1.1.3.1.1  设计指引与准则（执行文件和标准等是否已列出)。

> **English:** Design guidance and standards (Whether the executable files and standards have been listed).

###### 5.1.1.3.1.2  初始方案的可行性评估报告。

a) 根据初步设计方案，确认工装功能、工装结构形式以及操作性否满足要求；

b) 零件与工装协调依据是否正确、工艺过程是否便捷、有效；

c) 材料选择是否合理，工装在各个使用工况下是否稳定并且变形可控。

> **English:** Assessment Report of the initial program feasibility.

> **English:** Confirm whether the tooling function, structure and operability meet the requirements according to the preliminary design scheme;

> **English:** Whether the coordination basis between parts and tooling is correct and whether the process is

> **English:** convenient and effective;

> **English:** Whether the material selection is reasonable and whether the tooling is stable and the deformation is

> **English:** controllable under various working conditions.

###### 5.1.1.3.1.3  工装零组件设计计划。

> **English:** Design plan of tooling components.

##### 5.1.1.3.2  关键设计评审(CDR)

关键设计评审是对详细设计阶段成果的评审，三维设计数模基本完成，并可支撑后续加工制造的开展。

> **English:** CDR is the review of the results of the detailed design stage. The 3D design digital simulation is

> **English:** basically completed and can support the development of subsequent processing and manufacturing. 5.1.1.3.2.1

> **English:** Whether the detailed functions and the detailed dimension accuracy of the tooling are clear.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.1  工装的详细功能和细节尺寸精度等是否明确。

###### 5.1.1.3.2.2  工装协调依据是否正确、容差分配是否合理。

> **English:** Weather the coordination basis of the tooling is correct and Weather tolerance distribution is

> **English:** reasonable.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.3  关键尺寸是否控制、是否可测量等。

> **English:** Weather the critical dimensions can be controlled and be measured.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.4  工装定位及上、下架方式是否合理。

> **English:** Weather tooling positioning, loading and unloading methods is reasonable.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.5  是否已编制装配工装、吊具、模具等的计算报告/应力分析报告。

> **English:** Weather the calculation and stress analysis report of assembly tooling, overhead mechanical equipment and die has been prepared.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.6  工装零组件是否全部设计完成。

> **English:** Weather the tooling's components design plan has been completed.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.7  三维设计数模结构树是否已标准化。

> **English:** Whether the 3D design digital analog structure tree has been standardized;

###### 5.1.1.3.2.8  标准件、非标件选择是否合理，是否已明确厂商、牌号。

> **English:** Whether the selection of standard parts and non-standard parts is reasonable, and whether the

> **English:** manufacturer and brand have been clarified.

###### 5.1.1.3.2.9  工装的加工制造和检测方案是否合理可行（重点控制关键制造工序，包含但不限如工装的焊接工艺、热处理工艺以及变形控制等）。需提供完整的加工制造方案和关键工序控制方法。

注：关键设计评审结论中可注明“已成熟的项目部分可以预先投产”的内容。

> **English:** Whether the processing, manufacturing and testing scheme of tooling is reasonable and feasible (focus on controlling key manufacturing processes, including but not limited to welding process, heat treatment process and deformation control of tooling).The whole processing, manufacturing scheme and key process control scheme should be provided.

> **English:** Note: the critical design review conclusion can indicate the content of "mature projects can be put into operation in advance".

##### 5.1.1.3.3  最终设计评审(FDR)

最终设计评审是对最终设计阶段成果的评审，并确定所有设计成果足够支持全面的生产、装配和采购，标志着设计工作的结束。

> **English:** FDR is the review of the results of the final design stage, and determines that all design results are

> **English:** sufficient to support comprehensive production, assembly and procurement, marking the end of the

> **English:** design work.

###### 5.1.1.3.3.1  确定工装设计过程中所有影响工装功能与可靠性问题均已解决。

> **English:** To confirm the functionality and reliability issues that affecting tooling in the stage of tooling design are all solved.

###### 5.1.1.3.3.2  确定试验（若需）已证明工装功能、可靠性、可维护性与寿命等符合要求。

> **English:** To confirm the functional, reliability, maintainability, life of tooling that proved by experiment (if necessary) can meet the requirements.

###### 5.1.1.3.3.3  确定工装所用材料能够满足制造等要求。

> **English:** To confirm tooling materials can meet manufacturing requirements.

###### 5.1.1.3.3.4  工装零组件设计工作等全部结束。

a) 已完成工装的二维图纸。确认工装图样是否规范，相关执行文件标准是否已列出；

b) 确认必要的的基准线、基准面、工具球号或测量点等基准是否已标注。确认工装的精度和重要公差配是否能够满足产品设计需求；

c) 确认使用说明、维护要求、定期检查、松散件清单等是否正确；

> **English:** Tooling components and parts design and other works are all finished.

> **English:** 2D drawing of tooling has been completed. Confirm whether the tooling drawings are standardized

> **English:** and whether the relevant executive documents and standards have been listed;

> **English:** Confirm the necessary datum lines, datum planes, tool ball numbers or measuring points have been

> **English:** marked. Confirm whether the accuracy and important tolerance of tooling can meet the requirements of product design;

> **English:** Confirm the operation instructions, maintenance requirements, regular inspection, loose parts list, etc. are correct;

#### 5.1.1.4  评审结论

##### 5.1.1.4.1  各阶段的评审均需以书面形式形成评审结论，评审结论需明确是否通过评审或有条件通过等。

> **English:** All stages of review should produce results in written form. The review conclusions should be determined whether to pass or conditionally pass.

##### 5.1.1.4.2  评审过程中制定的行动项目需签字确认。

> **English:** Action items developed in the review process should require the airframe supplier to affirm and sign.

##### 5.1.1.4.3  针对评审过程中的行动项目应进行逐项落实以作为下阶段评审的输入资料。

> **English:** Airframe suppliers should fulfill the action items that developed in the review process as the output information in following review stages.

##### 5.1.1.4.4  各评审阶段所有输出的文件资料，均须以原件扫描件（PDF）的形式提供给中国商飞。

> **English:** Airframe suppliers should provide all output information in form of PDF at various review stages for COMAC.

##### 5.1.1.4.5  在完成并通过最终设计评审（100%批准）后方可全面开展生产制造任务。

> **English:** The manufacturing task can be fully carried out only after the final design review (100% approval) is completed and passed.

### 5.1.2  审批

#### 5.1.2.1  内部审批

内部审批按GLC14070202执行。

> **English:** Internal Approval according to GLC14070202.

#### 5.1.2.2  厂际审批

中国商飞控制工装项目评审通过后按下列要求进行审批：

a）在完成所有工装图样/数据的详细设计后提交审批前，工装设计室

应事先将评审/审批申请报告（附录 A）及审批资料通过数据管理平台或光盘等媒介提交中国商飞审批，审批资料至少应包括：工装设计技术条件等设计输入文件、工装设计相关执行文件标准、工装图样/数据/报告等设计输出文件。

b）中国商飞依据工装图样/数据的设计内容出具审批意见（50%、75%、95%或100%接受）并以备忘录形式反馈给沈飞民机。

c）除非备忘录给予100%接受的明确答复，否则科技创新部需按审批意见完善设计后再次提交中国商飞审批。

d）工装图样/数据经中国商飞审批后，工装设计室应按审批意见的内容，完成后续相应的设计工作。

e）当工装图样/数据经中国商飞100%接受后，原则上工艺装备室按自己的生产流程开始工装的制造，但工装评审结论、工装审批意见等文件中已有工装制造的要求时除外。

> **English:** The tooling controlled by COMAC should be approved according to the following requirements after the tooling passes the review:

> **English:** Before the approval, tooling design office should send the review/endorse application report (Appendix A) and approval material to COMAC through data platform or by disc after finishing the detail design of all tooling drawing/data. The approval material should at least include: design input files such as tooling order, related standards for tooling design, and design output files such as tooling drawing/data/reports, etc.

> **English:** COMAC issues the approval opinions (50%, 75%, 95% or 100% acceptance) according to the tooling drawing/data of the supplier and gives feedback to the SACC by using the memorandum.

> **English:** The Technology & Informatization Department should improve the design and submit to COMAC for approval again, unless the reply is 100% accepted on the memorandum.

> **English:** After the approval of COMAC, the tooling design office should complete the subsequent design work according to the approval opinions

> **English:** The tooling office can commence the tooling fabrication according to SACC's manufacturing process in principle when COMAC accepts the tooling drawing/date 100%. Except that there is requirements for tooling fabrication in the files of tooling review conclusions and tooling approval opinions etc.

## 5.2工装设计

### 5.2.1  设计要求

#### 5.2.1.1  工装设计除应满足国家法律法规，工程/工艺等要求外，还应满足本程序中规定的标准。

> **English:** Tooling Design should be in accordance with the law and rules of China and engineering/process requirements. Moreover, tooling design should satisfy the criterion prescribed in this document.

#### 5.2.1.2  工装设计输入文件(如工装设计技术条件、工装技术要求等)或工装订货（申请）单等应明确注明工程数模版次。

> **English:** The input file of Tooling Design(tool design request、tool technology request tc) or Tool Order must indicate the revision of drawing model.

#### 5.2.1.3  基准的选择要达到工艺基准和设计基准的统一。在基准转换时能确保产品的精度和装配准确度。

> **English:** Datum selection should follow the principle of the unification of process datum and design datum. Product precision and assembly accuracy should be ensured when the datum is shifted.

#### 5.2.1.4  制造依据正确，确保有关工艺装备之间相互协调。需要用标工（量规、样件等）作为协调依据的生产工装，应在工装图样上标明该工装的协调依据。

> **English:** Manufacturing basis should be right and can ensure the coordination of relevant tooling. The coordination basis of the tooling should be marked on the tooling drawing if standard tooling (gauge, sample piece, etc) is required.

#### 5.2.1.5  Ⅰ类和Ⅱ类工装图样上应该标出必需的基准线、基准面、工具球号或测量点等信息，以便于工装制造和定期检验。

> **English:** The necessary datum line, datum plane, reference ball or measurement points should be marked on the drawings of Category I tooling and Category II tooling for which is convenient for tooling manufacturing and periodic check.

#### 5.2.1.6  生产互换/替换件的工装，设计中应充分考虑工装能否符合

互换/替换要求。

> **English:** During the design of tooling used for the fabrication of interchangeable parts/replacements, the conformity of the interchanging/replacing requirements should be considered adequately.

#### 5.2.1.7  运输托架、包装箱在设计中应充分考虑运输方式、产品运输安全、吊装等因素。

> **English:** For transportation rack and wrapping box, the transportation way, product safety and hoisting issues, etc should be considered fully during the tooling design.

#### 5.2.1.8  工装设计应对定期检验项目、依据、周期等做出规定（其中工装图样中应明确是否定检、周期等要求）。

> **English:** For periodic check, the tooling design department should specify the tooling item, the check basis and the check period (tooling drawings should include the requirements of whether the tooling needs periodic check or not and check period, etc).

#### 5.2.1.9  工装设计应对工装标识、表面涂饰等要求做出规定。

> **English:** The tooling design office should make regulations for the requirements of tooling identification and the surface coating of tooling.

#### 5.2.1.10  C919项目的工装设计使用CATIA V5 R18版本；ARJ21项目的工装设计使用CATIA V5 R21版本；C929项目的系统件相关的工装（含PTE）设计使用CATIA R2020x，其他工装使用CATIA V5 R30。

> **English:** CATIA V5 R18 should be used for the tooling design in the C919 program. CATIA V5 R21 should be used for the tooling design in the ARJ21 program. CATIA R2020x should be used for tooling design related to system components (including PTE) and CATIA V5 R30 should be used for other tooling in the CR929 program.

#### 5.2.1.11  使用CATIA V5软件进行工装三维建模时的通用环境设置，坐标系相关规定、零件数模的比例、尺寸及单位要求、建模的基本原则和建模的一般要求、数字化数据集的命名要求应贯彻现行的行业标准及公司企业标准的规定。

> **English:** When design tooling with CATIA V5 environment setting, coordinate system, model rate, dimension and unit requirement, basic principle and general requirement for modeling, name rule of digital data set, , should follow the latest industrial standard and corporate

### 5.2.2  工装设计更改

#### 5.2.2.1  工装设计更改原则上需满足谁设计谁更改，若要委托更改，需有书面授权证明。

> **English:** The tooling deign change should follow the principle of "who designs who changes" and the authorization document should be included if the tooling needs to be changed by another designer.

#### 5.2.2.2  工装设计室制定工装设计的更改程序GLC14070202，当工装发生实质性更改后，工装版次需进行升版，以满足工装设计更改的控制要求。

> **English:** Tooling design office establishes the procedure of tooling design change GLC14070202 to satisfy the control requirements of the tooling design change. When the tool change is essential, the tool revision must upgrade.

#### 5.2.2.3  中国商飞控制工装项目的更改需报中国商飞审批，更改的工装图样/数据提交流程同5.1.2.2

a），中国商飞依据更改的情况确定是否需要再次组织评审，审批流程同5.1.2.2 b）、c）、d）、e），未经中国商飞审批同意，中国商飞控制工装项目的更改均不得用于产品的制造。

> **English:** The change of the tooling controlled by COMAC needs to be examined and approved by COMAC， and the changed tooling drawing/data submission procedures shall be the same as 5.1.2.2 a), COMAC decides whether another review is required to be organized or not according to the change situation，the approval process is the same as 5.1.2.2 b）、c）、d）、e），Without the permission of COMAC, the changed tooling controlled by COMAC could not be used to manufacture aircraft product.

### 5.2.3  工装设计文件控制

#### 5.2.3.1  正式发布的工装图样/数据等文件的传递由档案管理部门统一管理。

> **English:** The delivering of the formally published tooling drawing/data should be managed by the archives office.

#### 5.2.3.2  档案管理部门应保证正式发布的工装图样/数据等为现行有效版本。

> **English:** Archives office should ensure that the formally issued tooling drawing/data is the active and valid version.

#### 5.2.3.3  档案管理部门应保存工装图样/数据的历次更改记录以保证工装设计的可追溯性。

> **English:** Archives office should conserve all the previous change records of the tooling drawing/data to guarantee the traceability of the tooling design.

#### 5.2.3.4  工艺装备室应保存工装设计技术条件等工装设计输入文件资料。

> **English:** Tooling office should conserve such tooling design inputs documents as tooling design request, etc.

#### 5.2.3.5  工装设计文件的保存年限为工装产品全寿命。

> **English:** The conservation years of the tooling design documents should run through the whole lifetime of the tooling.

#### 5.2.3.6  当中国商飞需要查阅工装图样时，档案管理部门应给予提供。

> **English:** Archives office should provide the tooling drawing when COMAC needs to refer to it.

#### 5.2.3.7  工装设计准则（要求或规范）文件清单及文件报中国商飞审核，工装设计准则（要求或规范）变更时也应报中国商飞审核。

> **English:** Provide a list of tooling design criteria (requirements or specifications) documents and submit them to COMAC for approval. When changes are made to tooling design criteria (requirements or specifications), they should also be submitted to COMAC for review.
