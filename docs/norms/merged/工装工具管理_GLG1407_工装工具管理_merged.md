# 4  机构与职责

## 4.1 科技创新部

科技创新部为工装工具的归口管理部门，主要职责如下：

a）负责公司工装、工具业务整体管理工作；

b）负责工装申请审批，工装设计，外协采购申请提出，负责组织工装制造、组织工装验收、组织工装定检、工装返修实施，负责工装日常使用及维护保养情况的监控管理工作。

c）负责工具申请的审签，工具维修、工具报废的技术鉴定及日常管理等工作。

> **English:** As the centralized management department for tooling and tools, the Department of Science and Technology Innovation has the following main responsibilities:a) Responsible for the overall management of the company's tooling and tool business;b) Responsible for tooling application approval, tooling design, submission of external procurement applications, organizing tooling manufacturing, tooling acceptance, regular tooling inspection, tooling repair implementation, and monitoring and management of the daily use and maintenance of tooling;c) Responsible for the review and approval of tool applications, technical appraisal of tool maintenance and scrapping, and daily management.

## 4.2 工装使用单位

工装使用单位负责工装工具申请、使用、维护保养管理工作，主要职责如下：

a）工装使用单位负责本单位使用工装品种申请、返修申请、工装验收、工装定检、工装日常使用及维护保养、资产管理、工装状态管控等工作；

b）负责本单位的工装工具申请、接收、检验、登账、使用借用及日常管理等工作。

> **English:** Tooling user units are responsible for the application, use, and maintenance management of tooling and tools, with the following main responsibilities:a) Responsible for the application for types of tooling used by the unit, repair applications, tooling acceptance, regular tooling inspection, daily use and maintenance of tooling, asset management, and tooling status control;b) Responsible for the application, receipt, inspection, registration, use, borrowing, and daily management of tooling and tools within the unit.

## 4.3 生产运营部

a）生产运营部负责工具采购的实施，工具采购状态的跟踪工作。

> **English:** a) Responsible for the implementation of tool procurement and tracking the status of tool procurement.

## 4.4 质量管理部

a）质量管理部负责量具的检定、维修及协调解决相关问题等工作。

> **English:** a) Responsible for the verification and maintenance of measuring tools and coordinating the resolution of related issues.

# 5  管理要求

## 5.1  工装工具策划要求

a）按照主阶段计划及制造总方案要求，为飞机产品实现所需的工装工具进行策划，确定工装工具所需品种和数量，制定采买预算；

b）按照制造总方案要求，以及工装测算表、工装选择系数和控制数、互换性/替换性计划、装配协调方案、工具采购原则编制工艺装备及工具品种表；

c）按照工装定检目录及工装定检实施情况，结合工装使用单位生产计

划安排，开展工装定检实施策划，下发工艺装备定期检修计划表。

> **English:** a) In accordance with the main phase plan and overall manufacturing plan requirements, plan the tooling and tools required for aircraft product realization, determine the types and quantities of tooling and tools needed, and formulate procurement budgets;b) Compile the list of tooling and tool types in accordance with the overall manufacturing plan requirements, as well as tooling calculation sheets, tooling selection coefficients and control numbers, interchangeability/replaceability plans, assembly coordination plans, and tool procurement principles;c) Based on the regular tooling inspection catalog, the implementation status of regular tooling inspections, and the production schedule of tooling user units, carry out planning for the implementation of regular tooling inspections and issue the Regular Tooling Inspection Schedule.

## 5.2 工装申请与制造要求

a）工装申请管理；

1 按照已批准的工艺装备品种表或工艺装备品种表更改单提出工艺装备申请。

2 应采用标准表格申请工装，其内容至少包括工装名称、工装号、数量。

b）工装设计与更改；

1 工装设计应遵从于工装生产计划、工装设计技术条件、工程文件或数据以及其它设计依据，满足设计和开发策划的要求。

2 工装设计应进行设计和开发输出管理，确保工装结构合理，满足工装使用、工装制造和安全要求以及其它产品实现的要求。

3 工装材料和成品选用应考虑成本，尽量选用标准件、成品件，提高工装标准化系数。工装设计应符合国家标准、行业标准、企业标准及顾客的特殊要求，满足设计和开发过程控制要求。

4 工装图纸应经校对、审核、批准后才能正式发放。必要时，应经标准化审查、使用单位和顾客会签，保证设计和开发更改管理要求。

> **English:** a) Tooling Application Management

> **English:** Submit tooling applications in accordance with the approved list of tooling types or change orders to the list of tooling types.

> **English:** Standard forms shall be used for tooling applications, which shall include at least the tooling name, tooling number, and quantity.

> **English:** b) Tooling Design and Modification

> **English:** Tooling design shall comply with the tooling production plan, tooling design technical conditions, engineering documents or data, and other design bases, and meet the requirements of design and development planning.

> **English:** Tooling design is a low-risk development process.

> **English:** For inspection tooling, standard tooling, assembly tooling, spreaders, debris removal equipment, product cleaning equipment, and test equipment, tooling certification documents shall be issued indicating the tooling name, number, status, etc.

> **English:** Tooling shall be designed in-house, with external design for insufficient resources, and tooling manufacturing shall be provided by external resources.

> **English:** Storage requirements for stored tooling shall be specified, including regular protection/status inspections.

> **English:** c) Management of Tooling Data

> **English:** Catalogs, temporary storage areas, and release areas shall be established in the computer system, and the catalogs shall indicate the version validity of the data sets. Permission control for storage and access to the storage areas and release areas shall be clearly defined.

> **English:** Updates to data sets shall be declared when released in the computer system.

> **English:** Data sets shall be approved before release or re-release after update.

> **English:** Data sets shall be backed up regularly, and the storage of backup data shall be secured.

> **English:** d) Tooling Manufacturing Management

> **English:** Tooling shall be manufactured in accordance with approved process procedures, national standards, industry standards, enterprise standards, manufacturing specifications, and approved design requirements.

> **English:** The characteristics of tooling drawings shall be checked to verify compliance with requirements.

> **English:** When necessary, the delivery of tooling shall be accompanied by measurement data of tooling datums and proof of deviation rejection handling.

> **English:** Tooling used for production process automation and control/monitoring shall be confirmed and maintained before being used in production.

> **English:** Inspection work shall be carried out based on valid tooling drawings. During tooling acceptance, relevant records or quality assurance records shall be completed, and qualified certification documents shall be issued after tooling acceptance.

## 5.3 工装运行管理

a)工艺装备使用

> **English:** a) Use of Tooling

> **English:** When tooling is transferred from the manufacturing unit to the user unit, there shall be written documents confirming the receipt of the tooling and its completeness.

> **English:** Tooling user units shall establish accounts for the received tooling for management purposes.

> **English:** Operation instructions shall be compiled for complex tooling such as component assembly jigs, inspection jigs, precision machining tables, and large-scale complex test benches.

> **English:** Necessary visual and functional inspections shall be carried out before the use of tooling. If any problems are found during the inspection, the use of the tooling shall be stopped or restricted, and obvious marks shall be made. At the same time, the fault shall be promptly reported to the technical office of the user unit.

> **English:** The use of tooling shall ensure that the function of the tooling is not damaged.

> **English:** Necessary protection shall be provided for tooling during storage and transportation. Measures shall be taken to restrict the unauthorized use and transportation of standard tooling.

> **English:** Customer-supplied tooling assets shall, in principle, be owned by the customer, and specific circumstances shall be implemented in accordance with the signed project contract. The use and management of customer-supplied tooling include the receipt, storage, borrowing, maintenance, use, regular inspection, asset management, and return of customer-supplied tooling.

> **English:** b) Regular Inspection of Tooling

> **English:** A catalog of regular tooling inspections shall be issued for tooling requiring regular inspections, specifying the scope and cycle of regular inspections.

> **English:** Technical requirement documents for regular inspections shall be issued for tooling requiring regular inspections, specifying the content of regular inspections, which shall be carried out accordingly.

> **English:** Regular tooling inspection plans shall be issued in accordance with the regular inspection cycle, specifying the time or number of aircraft for regular tooling inspections, which shall be carried out as planned.

> **English:** Tooling shared by production and inspection shall be calibrated or verified as qualified before being used for inspection, and records shall be retained.

> **English:** The calibration of tooling shared by production and inspection before use for inspection shall ensure that the calibrated characteristics do not change during use or that the change is not sufficient to affect the product inspection process.

> **English:** c) Rework of Tooling

> **English:** Rework of tooling physical objects shall be carried out in accordance with the latest valid tooling drawings.

> **English:** When necessary, the characteristics of the reworked tooling drawings shall be checked to verify compliance with requirements.

> **English:** When necessary, the delivery of tooling shall be accompanied by measurement data of tooling datums and proof of deviation rejection handling.

> **English:** d) Maintenance of Tooling

> **English:** Tooling shall be regularly maintained and inspected to ensure its completeness and good condition.

> **English:** When tooling is not used for a long time, it shall be sealed.

> **English:** Before each start of work, tooling users shall inspect the completeness (whether the hole position bushings are firm and not missing), appearance, tightness, use function, safety measures, etc., of the tooling used.

> **English:** The Tooling Room shall organize physical verification of tooling. Tooling user units shall inspect tooling for wear and damage to ensure its completeness and good condition.

> **English:** Tooling user units shall use tooling correctly. If any faults or abnormalities are found, the use of the tooling shall be stopped immediately. Inspections for tooling wear and damage shall be carried out to ensure its completeness and good condition. If tooling wear or damage requires maintenance assistance from the Tooling Room, an application may be submitted. After technical appraisal by the technicians of the Tooling Room, a work order shall be issued for maintenance.

## 5.4 工装验证

a) 工装投入使用前应开展工艺装备验证，在验证过程中如发现问题，应停止使用或限用，并明显标识，同时应及时将故障反馈给工艺装备室。

b) 必须对新制及效率工装进行工装验证工作。

c) 对于返工/返修的工装，必要时对其进行部分或全部的工装验证。

d) 当用做检验目的工装其产品特性不能直接按工程依据验收时，须经过至少三架份的装配实践来验证工装。

> **English:** a) Tooling verification shall be carried out before tooling is put into use. If any problems are found during the verification, the use of the tooling shall be stopped or restricted, and obvious marks shall be made. At the same time, the fault shall be promptly reported to the Tooling Room.b) Tooling verification must be carried out for newly manufactured and efficient tooling.c) For reworked/modified tooling, partial or full tooling verification shall be carried out when necessary.d) When the product characteristics of tooling used for inspection purposes cannot be directly accepted in accordance with engineering bases, the tooling must be verified through the assembly practice of at least three aircraft.

> **English:** a) In accordance with the requirements of the main schedule and capacity improvement, combined with the tooling calculation sheet, put forward requirements for optimizing the quantity of tooling.b) For tooling scheme optimization found during tooling use, verification, and product production, put forward technical conditions for tooling optimization and carry out tooling optimization and version upgrade.
