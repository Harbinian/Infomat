# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装图号。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling drawing number.

（2）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set)".

（3）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装交检的类型，在相应的方框内打勾。对于非定检工装搬迁，在“返修”栏内打勾。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the type of tooling inspection and tick the corresponding column. For relocation of Non-PTI tooling, tick the rework column.

（4）：工装供应商交检人员填写派工号。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the working number.

（5）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装系列号。如无工装系列号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in tooling serial number. If there is no tooling serial number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（6）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装版次。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling revision.

（7）：工装供应商交检人员填写工装的检查结果，在相应的方框内打勾。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier fills in the tooling inspection results and tick the corresponding boxes.

（8）：工装供应商交检人员和/或科技创新部工艺装备室技术员/检验人员按需填写备注，包括：工装不具备交检条件的特性及其它想要特别说明的信息。

Inspection personnel of the tooling supplier and/or technician/inspector in Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department fills in the remarks as needed, including the unqualified characteristics in the tooling inspection results and other information to be specially described.

（9）：工装供应商交检人员签字并注明日期。

The tooling supplier's inspection personnel shall sign and indicate the date.

（10）：对于交付至公司使用的工装，科技创新部工艺装备室技术员签字并注明日期。对于交付至沈飞/供应商使用的工装，科技创新部工艺装备室工装检验人员签字并注明日期。

When tooling is delivered to company for using, the technician in Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department shall sign and indicate the date. When tooling is delivered to SAC/supplier for using, the tooling inspector in Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department shall sign and indicate the date.
