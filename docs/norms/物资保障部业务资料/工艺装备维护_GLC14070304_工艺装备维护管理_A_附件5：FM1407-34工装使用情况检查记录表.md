填写说明Instructions for Filling：

此表格要求在生产开工前及完工后按要求完成填写，研制项目或月产不满足一架份或一批次的，至少每月完成一次点检记录。

This form is required to be completed as required before and after production starts. If the development project or monthly production does not meet the requirements of one shelf or batch, at least one monthly inspection record should be completed.

工装负责人填写产品机型代号，如， C919 、A220。

The person in charge of the tooling fills in the product model code, such as S5A and S3B.

工装负责人填写使用单位，如35厂，39厂。

The person in charge of the tooling should fill in the unit of use, such as 35 factories and 39 factories.

（3）工装负责人填写工装图号。

Fill in the Tooling drawing No.

（4）工装负责人填写工装名称。

Fill in the Tooling name.

（5）工装点检维护人填写批架次，按批架次开工前对工装进行点检，完工后对工装进行点检、维护、保养。

The fixture inspection and maintenance personnel shall fill in the batch number, conduct spot checks on the fixture before starting work according to the batch number, and conduct spot checks, maintenance, and upkeep on the fixture after completion.

（6）工装点检维护人填写开工前点检时间，完工后点检维护保养时间。

The tooling inspection and maintenance personnel shall fill in the pre construction inspection time and the post completion inspection and maintenance time.

（7）~（12）工装点检维护人填写点检内容完好状态，缺失、损坏的填写（14）。

The maintenance personnel for tooling inspection shall fill in the inspection content in good condition, and fill in the missing or damaged items (14).

（13）维护保养人员按照维护保养计划填写维护保养完成时间。

Maintenance personnel shall fill in the completion time of maintenance according to the maintenance plan.

（14）工装点检维护人填写点检内容问题具体描述。

The maintenance personnel for tooling inspection fill in a specific description of the inspection content.

（15）工装点检人签字或盖章。

Signature or seal of the fixture inspector.

（16）无问题工装负责检验人员签字或盖章，存在问题的问题关闭后检验人员签字或盖章。

The inspector in charge of the problem free tooling shall sign or stamp, and the inspector shall sign or stamp after the problem is closed.
