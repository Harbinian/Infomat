# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装检验人员填写工装图号。

Tooling inspector fills in tooling drawing number.

（2）：工装检验人员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”。

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set) or the second set XX tooling".

（3）：工装检验人员填写派工号。

Tooling inspector fills in the working number.

（4）：工装检验人员填写工装系列号。如工装无系列号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling serial number. If the tooling has no serial number, fill in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（5）：工装检验人员填写所测工装零组件的版次。如同一页中记录不同版次的工装零组件结果，此栏填写工装的整体版次。

Tooling inspector fills in the version of the tested tooling components. As the results of tooling parts and components of different versions are recorded on the same page, the overall version of tooling is filled in this column.

（6）：工装检验人员按测量项填写序号。

Tooling inspector fills in the serial number according to the measured items.

（7）：工装检验人员填写测量件的件号或其在工装图纸中的页次信息。

Tooling inspector fills in the part number of the measuring piece or its page information in the tooling drawing.

（8）：工装检验人员填写测量件的数量。

Tooling inspector fills in the number of measuring pieces.

（9）：工装检验人员填写测量件在工装图纸中规定的尺寸、特性。如使用标准工装，此栏填写标准工装的图号、安装标准工装所用销棒直径的理论值以及其它工装图纸中规定的标准工装的相关特性。

Tooling inspector fills in the dimensions and characteristics of measuring pieces specified in the tooling drawing. If using master tooling, fill master tooling number, the theoretical value of diameter of the pin used in tooling assembling for master tooling and other relevant characteristics about master tooling specified in tooling drawing in the column.

（10）：工装检验人员填写测量件的测量结果。

Tooling inspector fills in the measurement results of the measuring piece.

如果使用通/止规等计量器具测量，此栏按测量结果填写“合格/不合格”，并写明使用的量具名称。

If measuring instruments such as go/no go gauges are used for measurement, this column shall be filled with "qualified/unqualified" according to the measurement results, and the name of the measuring instrument used shall be indicated.

如使用计数器具测量，此栏填写测量数值，每栏填写一种测量值，多个测量结果时将测量值向下方的栏目排列。对于多个测量件有同一测量结果的情况，可在数值旁注明该结果测量件的数量，如“3-XXX”（XXX代表测量值）。对于单个特性进行多次测量的情况，可填写测量值的范围，如“1.001mm~1.003mm”。

If a counting instrument is used for measurement, the measured value shall be filled in this column, and each column shall be filled in with one measurement value. When there are multiple measurement results, the measured values shall be arranged in the lower column. If multiple measuring pieces have the same measurement result, the number of measuring pieces with this result can be indicated beside the value, such as "3-XXX" (XXX represents the measured value). For multiple measurements of a single characteristic, the range of measured values can be filled in, such as "1.001mm~1.003mm".

如使用标准工装测量，此栏填写标准工装的图号、安装标准工装的销棒是否可以灵活转动以及销棒的直径测量值。当（9）栏中有其它测量特性时，填写相应的测量值。

If using master tooling for measurement, master tooling number, the result of flexible rotation of the pin to assemble tooling and pin diameter shall be filled in the column. When there are other measurement characteristics in column (9), relevant value shall be filled in the column.

如使用水准仪或激光跟踪仪等设备测量工装的水平和/或垂直，此栏填写水准仪或激光跟踪仪等设备是否正确设置的信息以及测量数值。

If using measurement device such as level gauge or laser tracker to measure tooling level and/or plumb, the result of measurement device correct set and measurement value shall be filled in the column.

（11）：如测量结果不合格，检验人员在此栏盖章并注明日期。

If the measurement result is unqualified, the inspector shall stamp and indicate the date in this column.

（12）：如测量结果合格，检验人员在此栏盖章并注明日期。

If the measurement result is qualified, the inspector shall stamp and indicate the date in this column.
