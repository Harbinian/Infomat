# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写该表格的编号，编号原则如下：

项目代号-D单位代码-流水号。

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the number of this form. The numbering principle is as follows:

Project code - D unit code - serial number.

（2）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写工装图号。

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the tooling drawing number.

（3）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set) or the second set XX tooling".

（4）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写工装版次。

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the tooling revision.

（5）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写工装系列号。如无工装系列号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the tooling serial number. If there is no tooling serial number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（6）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员填写使用不合格工装对产品造成影响的评估结果。

Technologist in the technical room of tooling user fills in the evaluation results of the impact of using unqualified tooling on products.

（7）：工装使用单位技术室工艺员签字并注明日期。

The technologist of the technical room of the tooling user shall sign and indicate the date.

（8）：工装使用单位技术室主任签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the director of the technical office of the tooling user.

（9）：科技创新部工艺装备室技术员填写不合格工装对产品造成影响的意见。

The technician in Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department shall fill in the opinions on the impact of unqualified tooling on products.

（10）：科技创新部工艺装备室技术员签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the technician in Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department.

（11）：科技创新部工艺装备室主管工程师签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the engineer in charge of Tooling Office of the Technology Innovation Department.

（12）：当不合格工装对产品造成影响时，质量工程室检验人员填写需要追溯的产品信息。

When the unqualified tooling has an impact on the product, the inspector of the Quality Engineering Office shall fill in the product information to be traced.

（13）：质量工程室检验人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the inspector of the Quality Engineering Office.

（14）：质量工程室主任签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the director of the Quality Engineering Office
