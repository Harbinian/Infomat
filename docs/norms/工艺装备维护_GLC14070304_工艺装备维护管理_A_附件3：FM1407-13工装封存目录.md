填写说明Instructions for Filling：

填写序号。

Fill in the serial number.

工艺员填写工装图号。

Fill in the Tooling drawing No.

工艺员填写工装名称。

Fill in the Tooling name.

工艺员填写工装制造单位。

The technician fills in the tooling manufacturing unit.

工艺员填写永久序列号。

The technician fills in the permanent serial number.

工艺员填写封存方式，如：就地封存、工装库封存等。

The technician shall fill in the sealing method, such as on-site sealing, tooling warehouse sealing, etc.

工艺员填写工装最后使用时间。

The technician fills in the final usage time of the tooling.

工艺员填写零、组件图号。

The technologist fills in the number of parts and component diagram drawings.

工艺员签字。

Signature of the technologist.

使用单位技术室主任签字。

Signed by the director of the technical department of the user unit.

使用单位技术副厂长签字。

Signed by the technical deputy director of the user unit.

项目工艺副总师签字。

Signed by the Deputy Chief Engineer of Project Process.

工艺装备室主管技术员签字，并维护相关目录。

The technical supervisor in charge of the tooling room shall sign and maintain relevant catalogs.

分发要求：使用单位、技术办公室、工艺装备室各一份。

Distribution requirements: One copy each for the user unit, technical office, and tooling room.
