填写说明Instructions for Filling：

（1）-（18）由工艺员填写。

(1) - (18) To be filled by the technician.

（1）填写产品项目名称，如A220、C919等。项目名称见MPG 040-02。

Fill in the product model name, such as A220、C919.

（2）填写工作包名称，如A220尾锥。

Fill in the unit, such as A220 TC.

（3）填写使用单位，如35厂，39厂。

Fill in the user unit, such as 35 factories and 39 factories.

（4）填写品种表类别。如，标准工装、装配工装、零件工装、地面设备、样板品种表。

Fill in the variety table category. For example, standard tooling, assembly tooling and sample variety table.

（5）填写品种表编号。

Fill in the variety table number.

如：项目代号－M×××××－×××

编号规则见MPG 040-02。

For example: project code - M ×××××－×××

See MPG 040-02 for numbering rules.

（6）填写更改单编号。

Fill in the Change No. of the variety table.

（7）填写序号。

Fill in the serial number.

（8）填写产品图号。

Fill in the aircraft drawing number.

（9）填写工装/样板名称。

Fill in the name of tooling/template.

（10）填写工装/样板图号。

Fill in tooling/sample drawing number.

（11）（12）（13）（14）按工装分批，填写工装数量。

Fill in the number of tooling in batches according to tooling.

（15）填写工装、样板是否为关键、重要工装。

Fill in whether the tooling and sample are critical or important tooling.

（16）填写工装是否为客供工装。

Fill in whether the tooling is provided by the customer.

（18）填写工装/样板备注信息，如具体技术要求等。

Fill in tooling/sample information, such as specific technical requirements.

（19） 当工装是标准工装时，技术办公室主管技术员签字；其他工装/样板，工装使用单位工艺员签字。

When the tooling is standard, the technician in charge of the technical office shall sign; Other tooling/templates shall be signed by the technologist of the tooling user.

（20） 当工装是标准工装时，技术办公室技术组长签字；其他工装/样板，工装使用单位技术室主任签字。

When the tooling is standard, the technical team leader of the technical office shall sign; Other tooling/samples shall be signed by the director of the technical office of the tooling user.

（21） 当工装是标准工装时，技术办公室副总工艺师签字；其他工装/样板，工装使用单位技术副厂长签字。

When the tooling is standard tooling, the deputy chief technologist of the technical office shall sign; Other toolings/templates shall be signed by the deputy technical director of the tool-using unit.

（22） 当工装是标准工装时，不填；其他工装/样板，技术办公室主管技术员签字。

When the tooling is standard tooling, do not fill in; Other tooling/samples shall be signed by the technician in charge of the technical office.

（23） 当工装是标准工装时/样板，不填；其他工装，技术办公室副总工艺师签字。

When the tooling is standard tooling/sample, do not fill in; Other tooling shall be signed by the deputy chief technologist of the technical office.

（24） 工装设计室设计员签字。

Signature of designer of tooling design room.

（25） 工艺装备室主管签字。

Signature of the director of the process equipment room.

（26）当工装是标准工装时，技术办公室总工艺师签字；其他工装/样板，技术办公室副总工艺师签字，如工装是大型装配工装和试验设备或有较大争议时，由项目副总师签字。

When the tooling is standard tooling, the chief technologist of the technical office shall sign; Other toolings/templates shall be signed by the deputy chief technologist of the technical office. If the toolings are large assembly toolings and test equipment or there are major disputes, they shall be signed by the deputy chief technologist of the project.

（27）如需会签项目签字。

If a project signature is required for co signing.

（28）申请者品种表版次，默认为NEW版，每更改一次，品种表升一次版本。

（29）申请者填写工装总数量。

（30）申请者填写本次更改数量，第一次填写更改数量为工装总数量。
