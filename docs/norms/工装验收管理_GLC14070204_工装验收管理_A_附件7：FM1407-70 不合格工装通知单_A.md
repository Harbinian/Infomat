# 填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装图号。

Inspector of Quality Engineering Room of the tooling user fills in the tooling drawing number.

（2）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”。

Inspector of Quality Engineering Room of the tooling user fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set)".

（3）：工装使用单位质量工程室检验人员填写工装验收时的不合格内容。

Inspector of Quality Engineering Room of the tooling user fills in the unqualified contents during tooling acceptance.

（4）：质量工程室检验人员签字。

Signature of the inspector of the Quality Engineering Office.

（5）：质量工程室检验人员注明签字日期。

The inspector of the Quality Engineering Office shall indicate the signature date.
