填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装使用单位人员填写工装备忘信息。信息中的工装如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”。

The personnel of the tooling user shall fill in the tooling memo information. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set) or the second set XX tooling".

（2）：工装使用单位人员填写工装使用单位名称。

The personnel of the tooling user shall fill in the name of the tooling user.

（3）：备忘信息填写人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the person filling in the memo.

（4）：工装使用单位检验人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the inspector of the tooling user.

（5）：工装使用单位质量副厂长/副主任签字并注明日期。

Signed and dated by the deputy quality director/deputy director of the tooling user.

（6）：工艺装备室技术员填写处理意见。

The technician in the process equipment room shall fill in the handling suggestions.

（7）：填写处理意见的人员签字并注明日期。

The person who fills in the handling opinion shall sign and indicate the date.

（8）：项目管理部项目经理/助理会签并注明日期。

Countersigned and dated by the Project Manager/Assistant of the Project Management Department.
