填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工艺装备室技术员填写工装使用单位名称。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the name of the unit using the tooling.

（2）：工艺装备室技术员填写年份和项目名称。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the year and project name.

（3）：工艺装备室技术员填写编号，按照DOPB143760中的技术文件编号原则编写。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the number according to the numbering principle of technical documents in DOPB143760.

（4）：工艺装备室技术员填写版次，按照DOPB143760中的技术文件版次管理原则填写。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the version according to the technical document version management principle in DOPB143760.

（5）：工艺装备室技术员填写页次信息。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the page information.

（6）：工艺装备室技术员填写序号。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the serial number.

（7）：工艺装备室技术员填写工装图号。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the tooling drawing number.

（8）：工艺装备室技术员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）或第二套XX工装”。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set) or the second set XX tooling".

（9）：工艺装备室技术员填写工装定检或定查的周期。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the periodic inspection or periodic check cycle of tooling.

（10）：工艺装备室技术员填写此栏。如工装执行定检，此栏填写“定检”。如工装执行定查，此栏填写“定查”。如此栏工装即执行定检又执行定查，此栏填写“定检/定查”。

Technician of Tooling Office fills this column. If the tooling is subject to periodic inspection, fill in "periodic inspection" in this column. If the tooling is subject to periodic check, fill in "periodic check" in this column. If the tooling in this column is subject to periodic inspection and periodic check, fill in " periodic inspection/periodic check" in this column.

（11）：如工装执行定检，工艺装备室技术员在相应的月份栏内填写“计划完成工装定检测量的日期”。如工装执行定查，工艺装备室技术员在相应的月份栏内画符号“/”。

If the tooling is subject to periodic inspection, fill in the corresponding month column with the "planned date of completion of the tooling periodic inspection measurement" by Technician of Tooling Office. If the tooling is subject to periodic inspection, draw the symbol "/" in the corresponding month column by Technician of Tooling Office.

（12）：工艺装备室技术员填写分发单位。

Technician of Tooling Office fills in the distribution unit.

（13）：编制人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and noted date by the preparer.

（14）：校对人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and noted date by the proofreader.

（15）：批准人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and noted date by the approver.

（16）：工装使用单位生产计划人员会签并注明日期。

Countersigned and noted date by the production planner of the tooling user.

（17）：项目管理部项目经理/助理会签并注明日期。

Countersigned and noted date by the Project Manager/Assistant of the Project Management Department.
