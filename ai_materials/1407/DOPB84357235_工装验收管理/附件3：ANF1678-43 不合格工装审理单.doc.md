填写说明 Instruction for Filling

（1）：工装检验人员填写工装图号。

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling drawing number.

（2）：工装检验人员填写工装名称。如为效率工装，填写工装的套数信息，如“XX工装（第2套）”

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling name. In case of duplicated tooling, fills in the number of sets of tooling, such as "XX tooling (the second set)".

（3）：工装检验人员填写工装所制造/检验产品的标识，如B777。

Tooling inspector fills in the model of the products manufactured/inspected by the tooling house，such as B777.

（4）：工装检验人员填写顾客工装图号。如无顾客工装图号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Tooling inspector fills in the customer tooling number. If there is no customer tooling number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（5）：工装检验人员填写工装系列号。如无工装系列号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling serial number. If there is no tooling serial number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（6）：工装检验人员填写工装套数号。

Tooling inspector fills in the tooling unit number.

（7）：工装检验人员填写工装所在地点。例如：39厂。

Tooling inspector fills in the location of tooling. For example, shop 39.

（8）：工装检验人员填写不合格工装所对应的派工号。

Tooling inspector fills in the working number corresponding to the unqualified tooling.

（9）：工装检验人员填写顾客工装套数号。如无顾客工装套数号，此栏填写“N/A”或“/”。

Tooling inspector fills in the customer unit number. If there is no customer unit number, fills in "N/A" or "/" in this column.

（10）：工装检验人员描述工装的不合格内容。提交客户审批时，需提供相应不合格工装的设计资料和实物照片。

Tooling inspector describes the unqualified contents of tooling. Relevant rejected tooling design data and tooling pictures shall be provide when customer review.

（11）：工装检验人员盖检验印章。

Tooling inspector stamps in this column.

（12）：工装检验人员注明盖章日期。

Tooling inspector shall indicate the date of stamp.

（13）：工装设计室设计员填写处置说明。

Designer in the Tooling Design Office fills in the disposal instructions.

（14）：处置人员签字并注明日期。

Signature of disposal personnel with date.

（15）：当顾客审批时，在此栏签字并注明日期。

When the sheet is reviewed by customer, signature of customer with date.

（16）：工装检验人员判定不合格工装是否已处置完成时，在相应栏内打“√”。

When the tooling inspector determines whether the unqualified tooling has been disposed, mark "√" in the corresponding column.

（17）：工装检验人员盖检验印章。

Tooling inspector shall stamp in this column.

（18）：工装检验人员填写盖章日期。

Tooling inspector shall fill in the date of stamp.
