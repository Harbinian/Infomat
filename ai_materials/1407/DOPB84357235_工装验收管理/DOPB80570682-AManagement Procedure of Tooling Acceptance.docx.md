Management Procedure of Tooling Acceptance

1  Scope

This management procedure specifies the acceptance requirements of fabricated, reworked and periodical inspection process equipment (hereinafter referred to as tooling), the management of unqualified tooling .

This management procedure is applicable to commercial aircraft production. 

This management procedure is developed according to the requirements of DOPA7805、D33200-1 and M1194.2  Normative Reference Documents        The following documents are essential for the application of this document. 6.2  Requirements for Tooling Acceptance Management

6.2.1  Tooling Office technician organizes delivery personnel to confirm if the tooling meet the acceptance conditions, the inspector of the tooling shall be organized on site to inspect the tooling and delivery documents. For the tooling with customer inspection points, Tooling Office technician coordinates with customers to complete the inspection of tooling objects and records. The tooling for programs below shall be accepted by customer tooling representative prior to first use. For other program, see management instruction of this management procedure.

(1) For Boeing program except for B787 program, new, relocated, reactivated, or modified category I and II tools shall be accepted by customer tooling representative, See D33200-1 for detail.

(2) For Airbus program, the tooling that customer accept design shall be accepted by customer tooling representative. See M1194 for detail.

6.2.2  The tooling user shall accept the tooling according to the tooling drawings, ANF3646-82, ANF2025-36 and other documents.

6.2.3  Tooling acceptance shall include but not limited to the following requirements:

6.2.3.1  Tooling Hardware

(1) The tooling shall be free of damage, missing parts and obvious corrosion.

(2) The tooling label and identification shall meet the requirements of DOPC552877-26.

(3) Tooling profile roughness satisfy the specification in tooling drawing.

6.2.3.2  Delivery Documents

(1) The completeness and accuracy of the contents in the delivery documents shall be ensured.

(2) The delivery documents include the following contents.

 a) Tooling Certificate 

 For newly-made tooling, ANF4248-13 is used for the qualification certificate of template and non periodic inspection tooling; ANF6181-73 shall be used for the qualification certificate of tooling to be inspected periodically; ANF0577-74 is used for qualification certificate of test equipment.

For repair, periodic inspection and relocated tooling, repair / periodic inspection/ relocation record shall be filled in the maintenance record of tooling certificate and sealed with inspection stamp.

 b) The following conventional measurements shall be recorded in ANF 1653-40.

Use measuring tools to measure positioning pin, positioning hole, drilling die hole and other dimensions with tolerance requirements. If the locating pin and bushing of tooling are standard parts, the certificate of standard parts can replace the measurement records. The certificate of standard parts should at least include the specification, quantity and inspection stamp of standard parts;

Install tooling parts according to master tooling. For tooling parts and components installed per master tooling, plug sheets specified in drawing should be used, inspection pins which diameter are accepted should be rotated flexibly. The temperature during using master tooling should be maintained between 17.7℃ to 25.5℃.

Use optical level/theodolite to measure the level and vertical of tooling.

  c) If digital measuring equipment is used to measure dimensions with tolerance requirements such as profile and hole position, digital measurement records shall be provided, which shall at least include: Tooling drawing number, Name, Tooling Version, Part Number (When measuring parts), Tooling MBD Number and Version  (Applicable to using model to measure tooling for Boeing program, the digital model number shall include the extension), Dispatch Number, Measurement Date, Serial Number, Name of datum and Measurement Point, Theoretical Value(Including XYZ), Tolerance, Actual Value(Including XYZ), Deviation, The Measurement Diagram of Profile and Hole Position, The Description of Temperature, Coordinate System and Measurement Content of The Measuring Part, and The Inspection Stamp. The requirements of laser tracker report see DOPC74646671-30. For measurement of tooling ball in periodical inspection, in addition to the laser tracker report, periodic inspection records of tooling shall be provided for B777 program, and ANF 5663-08 shall be provided for other programs.

d) If the tooling drawing has provisions on heat treatment, surface treatment and stress relief, corresponding inspection records shall be provided, which shall at least include tooling Drawing Number, Part Number, Work Number, Applicable Serial Number, Process Description, Inspection Stamp and Date.

e) For lifting tooling, load test report shall be provided. For lifting tooling with critical welding area in Boeing program, NDT test report shall also be provided. The report shall at least include Tooling Drawing Number, Test Data, Test Results, Work Number, Applicable Serial Number, Inspection Stamp / Signature and Date.

f) For composite tooling with air tightness requirements, air tightness test report shall be provided. The report shall at least include Tooling Drawing Number, Test Data, Test Results, Work Number, Applicable Serial Number, Inspection Stamp and Date.

  g) ANF8627-36 is delivered with tooling certificate and above record.

h) For delivery files of duplicated tooling, the unit information shall be contained in delivery files, such as "The second unit XXX tooling" or "XXX tooling(Unit 2)".

6.2.3.3  The acceptance personnel of tooling shall supervise and inspect the manufacturing, measurement of tooling. The requirements of tooling inspection and measurement as blow.

(1) For the features with tolerance which are noted in tooling drawing or file, proper measuring tool or device should be used to complete measurement. The maximum allowable error of measuring tool or device should not exceed 1/4 of tolerance of feature to be measured.

(2) For trials that are specified in tooling drawing or relevant files should be completed per specified parameters.

(3) For tooling parts and components installed per master tooling, plug sheets specified in drawing should be used, inspection pins which diameter are accepted should be rotated flexibly. The temperature during using master tooling should be maintained between 17.7℃ to 25.5℃.

(4) For tooling level and plumb measured by theodolite, theodolite should be set properly per handling specification.

(5) Before inspecting and measuring detachable tooling parts or components, the interface should be without rag and FOD.

(6) When the measurement unit is "inch", the measurement shall be accurate to 4 decimal places. Where the unit of measurement is "mm", the measurement shall be accurate to 3 decimal places. When the measurement unit is "deg", the measurement shall be accurate to 3 decimal places. The temperature (℃ or ℉) applied during measurement shall be accurate to 1 decimal place.

6.2.4  Management of Unqualified Tooling 

6.2.4.1 If one of the following conditions occurs in the tooling, it is the unqualified tooling:

a) The periodic inspection validity period has exceeded the specified;

b) Damaged or missing of tooling label /identification;

c) The parts are damaged, rusted or their integrity has been damaged;

d) The function does not meet the use requirements;

e) Damage to the protective device;

f) The tooling characteristics are not in conformity with the valid drawings or technical documents.

g) Unqualified tooling for verification.

6.2.4.2  The non-conforming tooling shall be identified immediately. Fill in ANF7072-57 by inspector in Quality Engineering Office of the tooling user, and stick ANF7072-57 on the tooling to indicate that it is forbidden to use. Transfer non-conforming tooling into MRCC or separate area to prevent misuse. After the tooling is disposed, it will be removed by inspector in Quality Engineering Office of the tooling user.

6.2.4.3  For the nonconforming tooling found in the process of tooling acceptance, tooling inspector in Quality Engineering Office of the tooling user shall fill in ANF1161-58 and feed back the nonconforming tooling to the tooling supplier.

6.2.4.4  Designer in Tooling Design Office receives ANF 1678-43 and propose disposition opinion. For the disposal of class I and II nonconforming tooling of Boeing program accepted as is, it is necessary to submit it to Boeing tooling designer by designer in Tooling Design Office, and the nonconforming tooling can be accepted as is after Boeing tooling designer approval.

6.2.4.5  Nonconforming tooling that has not been disposed or without disposition is not allowed to be handed over for inspection.

6.3.4.6  For unqualified tooling during using tooling, application for configuration change, rework, repair, scrap, replenished manufacture shall be done per DOPB60727412 / DOPB71758787.

6.2.4.7  When the tooling user finds that the size or other characteristic of the used tooling is nonconforming, it is necessary to evaluate whether the use of nonconforming tooling affects the product assembly, manufacturing or inspection results. The planner in Technical Office of the tooling user fills in the evaluation conclusion in ANF2770-40, and technician in Tooling Office fills in the comments. When the nonconforming tooling will not affect the products, it is not necessary to trace the relevant products. Otherwise, inspector in Quality Engineering Office of the tooling user fills in the products to be traced in ANF2770-40, and the tooling user reports ANF2770-40 to Quality & Safety Department, disposition shall be performed per DOPB41645587.
