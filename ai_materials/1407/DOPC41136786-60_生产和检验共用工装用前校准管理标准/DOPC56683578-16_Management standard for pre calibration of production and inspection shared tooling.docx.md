Management standard for pre calibration of production and inspection shared tooling

1  Scope

This document specifies the calibration content, methods, and requirements for production and inspection shared process equipment (referred to as shared fixtures) before inspection..

This document applies to all project production management of the company.

This document is developed based on DOPB06132724.

4  Terms and definitions

 

4.1 Shared Tooling

The tooling used for both processing and manufacturing products, as well as inspecting and accepting products, is called shared tooling.

4.2 Calibration before shared tooling inspection

When using shared fixtures for inspection, in order to prove that they can be used as a basis for checking whether the product is qualified, simple and practical effective methods and means (such as visual inspection, appearance inspection, etc.) are used for verification before use.

5  Regulations

5.1 Calibration Requirements

5.1.1 Common fixtures used for sheet metal, machining, welding, non-metallic, and component assembly, such as tapping inspection molds, filing inspection molds (fixtures), matching inspection fixtures, welding inspection fixtures, and assembly fixtures shared for production and inspection, should undergo calibration work before inspection (before each batch of parts manufacturing fixtures is used; before each unit of component assembly fixtures is used).

5.2 Calibration Methods and Content

Before using the shared tooling, the inspection personnel of the tooling using unit shall calibrate the tooling using the ANF8776-022 shared tooling pre inspection calibration table. Only after passing the calibration can it be used.

5.2.1 Appearance calibration

(1) Whether the shared tooling is complete and whether there are any missing parts;

(2) Whether the shared tooling is damaged, whether there are pressure pits, deformations, wear, and cracks;

(3) Whether the movable parts of the shared tooling are flexible and whether they are coated with oil for rust prevention;

(4) Whether the edge lines and other marking lines of shared tooling parts are clear and intact.

5.2.2 Batch Calibration

Are there any issues with the shared tooling that may affect the assembly of the previous batch of parts, components, and assemblies? If so, have the tooling problems been resolved.

5.2.3 Regular Inspection and Calibration

According to the management procedure DOPB14760685, whether the shared fixtures have undergone calibration checks and whether the records are complete.

5.3 Calibration Process

The technical staff of the using unit shall prepare the ANF8814-71 Civil Aircraft Calibration Shared Tooling Variety Table. Before using the tooling for each batch (rack), the inspector of the using unit shall inspect the physical tooling according to the ANF8814-71 Civil Aircraft Calibration Shared Tooling Variety Table. After passing the inspection, the inspector of the using unit shall fill out the ANF8776-022 Shared Tooling Pre Inspection Calibration Table and archive it for future reference. If the inspection is not qualified, the technical personnel of the using unit's technical room shall propose tooling repair according to DOPB71758787.
