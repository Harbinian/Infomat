Management Instruction for Tooling Periodic Inspection for ORGB Program

Scope

This management instruction specifies regulations of tooling periodic inspection.

This management instruction is applicable to ORGB program production.

This management instruction is developed in accordance with the requirements DOPB14760685 and SR0006.

Regulations

5.1  The technician in Tooling Office prepares and sets the periodic inspection tooling catalog with reference to "ORGB Control Tooling Project Catalog".

5.2 

For the tooling required for periodic inspection in the ORGB Control Tooling program, the technician in Tooling Office will submit the tooling periodic inspection plan (SAMC 04-58(S)) to the ORGB representative for approval after the design review is passed. The contents of the periodic inspection plan shall at least include the elements and methods of periodic inspection and the tools used in the process of periodic inspection. Any subsequent changes to the tooling periodic inspection plan shall be resubmitted to ORGB representatives for approval and the tooling periodic inspection work shall be carried out in accordance with the periodic inspection plan approved by ORGB representatives.

5.3  Tooling Periodic Inspection Requirements at The Development and Batch Stage

5.3.1  

When the following situations occur, it is necessary to check the periodic inspection tooling in the ORGB control tooling project.

a) Development stage of the assembly tooling before each unit starts.

b) If the start date of the first assembly is more than 30 days after the delivery date of the tooling (including new production, major change of repair, and change of installation location).

c) Restart when the deactivation date exceeds the periodic inspection period.

5.3.2  For the periodic inspection tooling with product inspection function, the maximum periodic inspection period shall not exceed 18 months.

5.4  Into the batch production stage, type II tooling, bearing a certain load of the product, lifting support joints and jacks, tooling with product inspection function and inspection samples (only used for product inspection, but not for manufacturing other tooling or directly processed products) must be periodic inspected and records of each periodic inspection should be kept. The maximum periodic inspection period of the tooling shall not exceed 24 months.

5.5  The accuracy of type II tooling may be affected by an earthquake of magnitude 5.0 or greater occurring within a radius of 80 km. The tooling datum must be immediately measured per periodical tooling inspection process, the nonconformance tooling must be reworked and re-measured, tooling can be used until qualified. If top 20% of tooling are qualified, the Tooling Office organize the department that perform measurement to apply for approval from a representative appointed by ORGB for exemption from inspection of the other tooling.

5.6 For inter-factory shared tooling that are periodically inspected according to "date" cycle, the inspector in Quality Engineering Office of Tooling User shall ensure if tooling are within validate date via checking tooling tag and submit ORGB quality representative for confirmation before using tooling . The inter-factory shared tooling that exceed validate date must not be used.

5.7  Usually ORGB shall be responsible for the inspection of inter-factory shared tooling. If ORGA is responsible for the inspection of inter-factory shared tooling, the quality representative of ORGB shall participate in the acceptance, the inspector in Quality Engineering Office of Tooling User shall coordinate with ORGB to fill in relevant tooling periodical inspection information. For inter-factory shared tooling that are periodically inspected according to "ship set" cycle, fill in the specific unit information used by the tooling on the periodic inspection (SAMC 05-50). The content and seal should be correct, complete, clear and not easy to erase. For signs made of metal or plastic material, steel marked should be used. The inspector in Quality Engineering Office of Tooling User shall verify that the above information is correct, complete and clear and submit it to ORGB quality representative for confirmation before using tooling.

6  Standard Legend Description

None

7  Record Control

7.1  Relevant records should be filled and maintained in accordance with procedure DOPB080008 requirements.

7.2  The tooling periodic inspection records should be kept for at least 10 years.

（1）：工艺装备室技术员填入适用的机型。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the appropriate model.

（2）：工艺装备室技术员填入要检查的工装图号。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the drawing number of tooling to be checked.

（3）：工艺装备室技术员填入要检查的工装名称。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the name of tooling to be checked.

（4）：编制人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and noted date by the preparer.

（5）：校对人员签字并注明日期。

Signed and noted date by the proofreader.

（6）：工艺装备室技术员填写计划下发的依据

The technician in Tooling Room fills out the distribution basis of the plan.

（7）：工艺装备室技术员填写工装定期检查架份数。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the certain amounts of aircrafts to be periodic checked.

（8）：工艺装备室技术员在工装日期周期检查阶段填写检查周期。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the inspection period during the tooling date cycle inspection phase.

（9）：工装检验人员盖检验印章并注明检验日期。

The tooling inspector seals the inspection and note date of inspection completion.

（10）：工艺装备室技术员填入要检查的工装图号。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the drawing number of tooling to be checked.

（11）：编制人员签字并注明日期。。

Signed and noted date by the preparer.

（12）：工艺装备室技术员按需填入顺序号，即“1、2、3……”

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the serial number as required, i.e.“1、2、3……”.

（13）：工艺装备室技术员按需填写工装图纸中检查部位的页次、图区。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the page number and area of the inspection parts in the tooling drawing as required.

（14）：工艺装备室技术员填写需要检验的内容。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the contents that need to be inspected.

（15）：工艺装备室技术员按需填写工装零件号。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the tooling part number as required.

（16）：工艺装备室技术员按需填入测量时所需工具、设备。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the necessary tools and equipments for measurement.

（17）：工艺装备室技术员按需填入检查后的记录形式、内容等。

The technician in Tooling Room fills in the record situation, contents, etc. after inspection as required.

Inspector seal.
