Foreword

This system is an operational management system.

This system is proposed and drafted by the Technology Innovation Department.

The Technology Innovation Department shall be responsible for the interpretation of this system.

1 Scope

This document specifies the process: after the completion of tooling planning and tooling budget approval, the user unit shall submit a Tooling Application Form, formulate tooling technical specifications, and complete the process review of the tooling technical specifications, ultimately meeting the application requirements and budget requirements proposed by the user unit. By establishing a process for the selection and application of tooling, it is used to standardize the application and approval procedures of tooling.

This procedure applies to all projects.

This procedure is formulated in accordance with the requirements of DOPA 7805.

4 Terms and Definitions

4.1 Standard Tooling

A rigid entity that reflects the geometric shape and dimensions of certain parts of a product at a 1:1 actual scale. It serves as an analog standard for manufacturing, inspection, and coordination of production tooling, and is an important basis for ensuring dimensional and shape coordination and interchangeability between production tooling and product parts/subassemblies. Examples include standard samples, standard gauges, standard models, joint templates, and auxiliary gauges.

4.2 Production Tooling

Tooling used for manufacturing, assembling, and installing products, such as jigs, precision machining tables, molds, forming dies, drilling jigs, milling fixtures, modular fixtures, inspection fixtures, ground equipment, and test equipment. Among them, Class Ⅱ Tooling includes assembly tooling for main final products, final product inspection fixtures, and reusable lifting equipment; the rest are Class Ⅲ Tooling.

4.3 Template

A dedicated rigid measuring tool manufactured based on mold lines or data, which represents the actual shape of aircraft parts/subassemblies, with marked markings and drilled process holes. Examples include contour templates, cutting/drilling templates, and chemical milling templates.

5 Organizations and Responsibilities

5.1 Tooling User Unit

5.1.1 The compiler of the Tooling Application Form shall be responsible for applying for production tooling and templates, and reviewing the correctness and compliance of the applications for production tooling and templates.

5.2 Technology Innovation Department

5.2.1 The Technical Office shall be responsible for reviewing the compliance, processability, quantity of tooling, and applications for standard tooling.

5.2.2 The Tooling Design Office shall be responsible for reviewing the applicability of tooling.

5.2.3 The Tooling Office shall be responsible for estimating the cost of the applied tooling.

5.2.4 The Tooling Office shall be responsible for estimating the manufacturing cycle of the tooling.

6.3 Tooling Application Management Requirements

6.3.1 The application for tooling (including efficiency tooling) shall use Form ANF 2728-71, and the application for templates shall use Form ANF 1165-71.

6.3.2 For Tooling Application Forms marked with "Buyer-Controlled Tooling (referred to as Controlled Tooling)", tooling technicians and tooling designers shall verify the controlled tooling catalog. In subsequent processes such as tooling design, manufacturing, delivery, and verification, they shall organize all units to implement customer requirements in accordance with customer documents, including design review, selection of customer inspection points for manufacturing process procedures, customer inspection, production verification registration, tooling deactivation registration, tooling scrapping approval, and other relevant customer requirements.

6.3.3 If the tooling/templates for left and right parts of a product cannot be shared, separate Tooling/Template Application Forms shall be submitted respectively.

6.3.4 Tooling/Template Application Forms shall be managed by edition, numbered in sequence as A, B, C... (I, O, Q, S, X, Z are not allowed). The first application shall be Edition A. When a Tooling/Template Application Form needs to be revised, the edition shall be upgraded, and the approval route for the upgraded form shall be the same as that of the original version. All editions of the Tooling Application Form shall be valid simultaneously; after the new version of the Template Application Form is approved and takes effect, the original version shall be invalidated. When applying for tooling, the edition of the engineering digital model shall be recorded after the model.

6.3.5 If tooling needs to be revised due to changes in aircraft engineering drawings, process methods, or other reasons, a Tooling Application Form shall be prepared. In the "Technical Requirements" column, the reasons and basis for the revision, the page numbers of the revised tooling drawings, the content of the revised parts, the structure form shall be specified, and a simplified diagram shall be attached. For tooling revisions caused by changes in aircraft engineering drawings, the part number, edition, and other contents of the revised product shall be listed.

6.3.6 To ensure that the technical requirements for tooling edition upgrade are comprehensive, if the previous edition upgrade of the tooling has been finalized but not completed, no new application for tooling edition upgrade shall be submitted.

6.3.7 If the previous edition upgrade of the tooling has not been finalized and signed, and the applicant from the user unit finds that the technical requirements need to be revised or added, a new application may be submitted. The technical specifications shall clearly state that the edition upgrade shall be merged with the previous application, and the requirements for revising the previous changes shall be put forward.

6.3.8 If the tooling needs to be canceled because the commercial contract has not been signed, the tooling variety list shall be revised first, then an application for tooling edition upgrade shall be submitted. The technical specifications shall clearly state the reasons for cancellation and the description of canceling tooling manufacturing. For tooling for which a manufacturing contract has been signed, the process engineer of the user unit shall submit an application for contract cancellation.

6.3.9 If the proposed tooling requirements need to be revised due to reasons such as adjustment of workshop assembly plan, adjustment of production rate, failure to remove the product from the shelf, tooling manufacturing, etc., the demander shall submit a higher-level application, and the revision shall be approved by the director of the Tooling Office.

6.3.10 During the development phase of a new aircraft, before applying for tooling, the project team shall jointly study the process plan. After the plan is determined, the user unit shall prepare the Tooling Application Form.

6.3.11 For newly manufactured tooling, the quality assurance period of the corresponding tooling shall be specified in the Tooling Application Form. The quality assurance period for assembly tooling is 30 aircraft units, and the quality assurance period for part tooling is 3 batches. If the quality assurance period is less than 1 year, it shall be specified as 1 year.

6.3.12 When submitting an application for new tooling or tooling edition upgrade, the tooling proposer/user unit shall fully consider the necessity and feasibility of the proposed requirements, as well as the application approval time (generally no more than 7 days), contract signing time (generally no more than 40 days), tooling manufacturing time (which varies according to specific work hours and can be consulted with the Tooling Office), and whether the on-site process layout has been approved, the time for site clearance and product removal from the shelf. For part tooling, the assembly units and demand time of the parts shall be fully considered.
