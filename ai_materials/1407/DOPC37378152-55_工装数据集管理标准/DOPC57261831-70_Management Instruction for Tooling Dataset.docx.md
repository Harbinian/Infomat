Management Instruction for Tooling Dataset

1  Scope

This management instruction regulates the source, numbering method, revision management and releasing method of tooling dataset.

This management instruction is applicable to commercial aircraft production.

This management instruction is developed according to the requirements of DOPB02846447.

2  Reference Documents

DOPB02846447   Management Procedure for Design and Change of Tooling

DOPB143760    Management requirement for Technical Document

DOPB080008    Management Procedure for Quality Records

3  Forms

None

4  Terms and Definitions

None

5  Responsibilities

5.1  Source of the tooling dataset

The tooling dataset mainly come from customers, Self-design, Translation Design, Translation and Duplication, etc.

5.2  Numbering method of the tooling dataset

Each tooling with independent functions shall have a separate tooling drawing number. The efficiency tooling drawing number is the same, but it should be distinguished by a separate serial number. Only one single number is available for every set of tooling drawings, with no repeated use allowed. An obsolete drawing number shall no longer be used.

5.3.1  

The codes of designing and requesting departments shall accord with Corporate Organizations Codes.

5.3.2  

Tooling Identifier: referred to the engineering drawing number and semicolon. It can also be other drawing numbers and semicolons as specified. Redesign of the same tool shall not obsolete the original tooling, and the tooling identifier is to be suffixed with A, B,C……

5.3.3  

Tooling Type Code: consisting of a group of letters indicating the function of this tooling or the type of work this tooling performs. It is usually the acronym of the English tooling nomenclature (refer to Appendix A).

5.3.4  

Tooling Purpose Prefix: indicating the purpose of the tooling (Refer to Appendix B).

5.3.5  

Tooling Sequential Number: When a series of tooling bear the same tooling type code, and are used in different operations of the same part, then the Tooling Sequential Number is supplied additionally to show the sequence of operations with 2, 3, 4……(Sequential No 1 is omitted). Example:

Tooling No. 1: FAJ5-50000-1

Tooling No. 2: 2FAJ5-50000-1

Tooling No. 3: 3FAJ5-50000-1

5.3.6  

The maximum total number of digits in any tooling sequence number, tooling purpose prefix and tooling type code shall not exceed 6 characters.

5.3.7  

Code of setting-up fixtures shall only be applicable to assembling the toolings by setting-up fixtures.

5.3.8  

Program code shall be in accordance with the requirements in Appendix A of DOPB143760, unless otherwise specified.

5.3.9  

Refer to Appendix C for examples of tooling drawing numbering.

5.4  Title blocks of Tooling Drawings

5.4.1  

The title block of the master drawing shall comply with the requirements of Appendix D, D1.

5.4.2  

The title block of the assembly drawing shall comply with the requirements of Appendix D, D2.

5.4.3  

The title block of the detail drawing shall comply with the requirements of Appendix D, D3.

5.4.4  

The title block of the electric drawing shall comply with the requirements of Appendix D, D4.

5.4.5  

It is necessary to discuss with Standardization department for modifying the format of the title block or increasing (or decreasing) the title block information.

5.5  Design requirements for tooling Dataset

5.5.1  

Three dimensional mould design of tooling Dataset should comply with the regulations of temporary industry standards and enterprise standards.

5.5.2  

The drafting requests, numbering principles and revision management of tooling technique documents should comply with the regulation in DOPB143760.

5.6  Management requirements for tooling Dataset

5.6.1  

Tooling drawing should comply with this procedure regulation.

5.6.2  

Tooling Dataset code should comply with this procedure regulation.

5.6.3  

The customers' requirements shall be respected if they have established special requirements for numbering of tools.

5.6.4  

Discussion with standardization department is necessary if increase (or decrease) the group code.

5.7  Modification of tooling Dataset

Modification of tooling drawing should comply with the regulation in DOPB02846447.

5.8  

Specified requirements for tooling drawing of B777 and B787 program

Specified title blocks style reference appendix E, increasing engineering dataset and edition information, the rest are in accordance with appendix D.

5.9  Tooling dataset revision Management

Management Tooling dataset revision according to DOPB02846447.

5.10  Tooling Data Release

Release Tooling Data according to DOPB02846447.

6  Standard Legend Description

None

7  Record control

7.1  

Fill and maintenance relevant records according to the requirements of procedure DOPB080008.
