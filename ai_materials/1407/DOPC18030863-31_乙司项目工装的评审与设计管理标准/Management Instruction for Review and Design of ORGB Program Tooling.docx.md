Management Instruction for Review and Design of ORGB Program Tooling

1  Scope

This management instruction specifies the requirements of tooling review and design.

This management instruction is applicable to AX001, AX02 and AX03 program.

This management instruction is developed according to DOPB02846447, SR0006, SR0008.

2  Referenced Documents

     DOPB080008   Management Procedure for Quality Records

     SR0006   Regulations of Tooling Design, Manufacturing, Acceptance and Management

     SR0008   Tooling Design Review of airframe supplier's tooling program that is controlled by ORGB

3  Form

None

4  Terms and Definitions

None

5  Regulations

5.1  Tooling Review and Approval

5.1.1  Review

5.1.1.1  Internal Review

Internal Review as per DOPB02846447.

5.1.1.2  Inter-plant Review

The tooling controlled by ORGB needs inter-plant review:

a) 

The review is organized by ORGB and undertaken by the tooling design department. ORGB, corresponding components manufacturers and related experts, designers participate in the review.

a) Before the review, tooling design department should send the review/endorse application report (Appendix A) and review material to ORGB through data platform or by disc after finishing the tooling scheme design. The material to be reviewed should include at least: design input files such as tooling order, related standards for tooling design, and design output files such as tooling drawing/data/reports, etc.

c）The review results should be recorded in written form and filed.

5.1.1.3  Review content

5.1.1.3.1  Preliminary Design Review(PDR)

PDR is the review of the results of the preliminary design stage and the completion of three-dimensional preliminary design. The purpose is to determine that the correct design scheme has been selected and can meet all process and functional requirements.

5.1.1.3.1.1  

Design guidance and standards (Whether the executable files and standards have been listed).

5.1.1.3.1.2  

Assessment Report of the initial program feasibility.

a) Confirm whether the tooling function, structure and operability meet the requirements according to the preliminary design scheme;

b) Whether the coordination basis between parts and tooling is correct and whether the process is

convenient and effective;

c) Whether the material selection is reasonable and whether the tooling is stable and the deformation is 

controllable under various working conditions.

5.1.1.3.1.3  

Design plan of tooling components.

5.1.1.3.2  Critical Design Review(CDR)

CDR is the review of the results of the detailed design stage. The 3D design digital simulation is 

basically completed and can support the development of subsequent processing and manufacturing. 5.1.1.3.2.1  

Whether the detailed functions and the detailed dimension accuracy of the tooling are clear.

5.1.1.3.2.2  

Weather the coordination basis of the tooling is correct and Weather tolerance distribution is 

reasonable.

5.1.1.3.2.3  

Weather the critical dimensions can be controlled and be measured.

5.1.1.3.2.4  

Weather tooling positioning, loading and unloading methods is reasonable.

5.1.1.3.2.5  

Weather the calculation and stress analysis report of assembly tooling, overhead mechanical equipment and die has been prepared.

5.1.1.3.2.6  

Weather the tooling's components design plan has been completed.

5.1.1.3.2.7  

Whether the 3D design digital analog structure tree has been standardized;

5.1.1.3.2.8  

Whether the selection of standard parts and non-standard parts is reasonable, and whether the 

manufacturer and brand have been clarified.

5.1.1.3.2.9  

Whether the processing, manufacturing and testing scheme of tooling is reasonable and feasible (focus on controlling key manufacturing processes, including but not limited to welding process, heat treatment process and deformation control of tooling).The whole processing, manufacturing scheme and key process control scheme should be provided.

Note: the critical design review conclusion can indicate the content of "mature projects can be put into operation in advance".

5.1.1.3.3  Final Design Review(FDR)

FDR is the review of the results of the final design stage, and determines that all design results are 

sufficient to support comprehensive production, assembly and procurement, marking the end of the 

design work.

5.1.1.3.3.1  

To confirm the functionality and reliability issues that affecting tooling in the stage of tooling design are all solved.

5.1.1.3.3.2  

To confirm the functional, reliability, maintainability, life of tooling that proved by experiment (if necessary) can meet the requirements.

5.1.1.3.3.3  

To confirm tooling materials can meet manufacturing requirements.

5.1.1.3.3.4  

Tooling components and parts design and other works are all finished.

a) 2D drawing of tooling has been completed. Confirm whether the tooling drawings are standardized 

and whether the relevant executive documents and standards have been listed;

b) Confirm the necessary datum lines, datum planes, tool ball numbers or measuring points have been 

marked. Confirm whether the accuracy and important tolerance of tooling can meet the requirements of product design;

c) Confirm the operation instructions, maintenance requirements, regular inspection, loose parts list, etc. are correct;

5.1.1.4  The review results

5.1.1.4.1  

All stages of review should produce results in written form. The review conclusions should be determined whether to pass or conditionally pass.

5.1.1.4.2  

Action items developed in the review process should require the airframe supplier to affirm and sign.

5.1.1.4.3  

Airframe suppliers should fulfill the action items that developed in the review process as the output information in following review stages.

5.1.1.4.4  

Airframe suppliers should provide all output information in form of PDF at various review stages for ORGB.

5.1.1.4.5  

The manufacturing task can be fully carried out only after the final design review (100% approval) is completed and passed.

5.1.2  Approval

5.1.2.1  Internal Approval

Internal Approval according to DOPB02846447.

5.1.2.2  Inter-plant Approval

The tooling controlled by ORGB should be approved according to the following requirements after the tooling passes the review:

a）Before the approval, tooling design office should send the review/endorse application report (Appendix A) and approval material to ORGB through data platform or by disc after finishing the detail design of all tooling drawing/data. The approval material should at least include: design input files such as tooling order, related standards for tooling design, and design output files such as tooling drawing/data/reports, etc.

b）ORGB issues the approval opinions (50%, 75%, 95% or 100% acceptance) according to the tooling drawing/data of the supplier and gives feedback to the ORGA by using the memorandum.

c)

The Technology & Informatization Department should improve the design and submit to ORGB for approval again, unless the reply is 100% accepted on the memorandum.

d)

After the approval of ORGB, the tooling design office should complete the subsequent design work according to the approval opinions

e)

The tooling office can commence the tooling fabrication according to ORGA's manufacturing process in principle when ORGB accepts the tooling drawing/date 100%. Except that there is requirements for tooling fabrication in the files of tooling review conclusions and tooling approval opinions etc.

5.2  Tooling design

5.2.1  Design Requirements

5.2.1.1  

Tooling Design should be in accordance with the law and rules of China and engineering/process requirements. Moreover, tooling design should satisfy the criterion prescribed in this document.

5.2.1.2  

The input file of Tooling Design(tool design request、tool technology request etc.) or Tool Order must indicate the revision of drawing model.

5.2.1.3  

Datum selection should follow the principle of the unification of process datum and design datum. Product precision and assembly accuracy should be ensured when the datum is shifted.

5.2.1.4  

Manufacturing basis should be right and can ensure the coordination of relevant tooling. The coordination basis of the tooling should be marked on the tooling drawing if standard tooling (gauge, sample piece, etc.) is required.

5.2.1.5  

The necessary datum line, datum plane, reference ball or measurement points should be marked on the drawings of Category I tooling and Category II tooling for which is convenient for tooling manufacturing and periodic check.

5.2.1.6  

During the design of tooling used for the fabrication of interchangeable parts/replacements, the conformity of the interchanging/replacing requirements should be considered adequately.

5.2.1.7  

For transportation rack and wrapping box, the transportation way, product safety and hoisting issues, etc. should be considered fully during the tooling design.

5.2.1.8  

For periodic check, the tooling design department should specify the tooling item, the check basis and the check period (tooling drawings should include the requirements of whether the tooling needs periodic check or not and check period, etc.).

5.2.1.9  

The tooling design office should make regulations for the requirements of tooling identification and the surface coating of tooling.

5.2.1.10  

CATIA V5 R18 should be used for the tooling design in the AX02 program. CATIA V5 R21 should be used for the tooling design in the AX001 program. CATIA R2020x should be used for tooling design related to system components (including PTE) and CATIA V5 R30 should be used for other tooling in the AX03 program.

5.2.1.11  

When design tooling with CATIA V5, the environment setting, coordinate system, model rate, dimension and unit requirement, basic principle and general requirement for modeling, name rule of digital data set, etc., should follow the latest industrial standard and corporate standards.

5.2.2  Tooling Design Change

5.2.2.1  

The tooling deign change should follow the principle of "who designs who changes" and the authorization document should be included if the tooling needs to be changed by another designer.

5.2.2.2  

Tooling design office establishes the procedure of tooling design change DOPB02846447 to satisfy the control requirements of the tooling design change. When the tool change is essential, the tool revision must upgrade.

5.2.2.3  

The change of the tooling controlled by ORGB needs to be examined and approved by ORGB， and the changed tooling drawing/data submission procedures shall be the same as 5.1.2.2 a), ORGB decides whether another review is required to be organized or not according to the change situation，the approval process is the same as 5.1.2.2 b）、c）、d）、e），Without the permission of ORGB, the changed tooling controlled by ORGB could not be used to manufacture aircraft product.

5.2.3  Tooling Design Document Control

5.2.3.1  

The delivering of the formally published tooling drawing/data should be managed by the archives office.

5.2.3.2  

Archives office should ensure that the formally issued tooling drawing/data is the active and valid version.

5.2.3.3  

Archives office should conserve all the previous change records of the tooling drawing/data to guarantee the traceability of the tooling design.

5.2.3.4  

Tooling office should conserve such tooling design inputs documents as tooling design request, etc.

5.2.3.5  

The conservation years of the tooling design documents should run through the whole lifetime of the tooling.

5.2.3.6  

Archives office should provide the tooling drawing when ORGB needs to refer to it.

5.2.3.7  

Provide a list of tooling design criteria (requirements or specifications) documents and submit them to ORGB for approval. When changes are made to tooling design criteria (requirements or specifications), they should also be submitted to ORGB for review.
