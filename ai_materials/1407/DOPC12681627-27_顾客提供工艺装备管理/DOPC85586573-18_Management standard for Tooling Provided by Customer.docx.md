Management standard for Tooling Provided by Customer

1  Scope

This  management standard  specifies the management and control of customer-supplied tooling.

This management standard is applicable to civil aircraft production.

This management standard is formulated according to the DOPB06132724 requirements.

4  Terms and Definitions

4.1 Customer-Supplied Tooling

It refers to master tooling, production tooling ，template and inspection tooling supplied by customer per contract requirements.

5  Regulations

5.1  Tooling Acceptance

5.1.1 The project Manager of Project Management shall notify Tooling Office when and where customer-supplied tooling will arrive in ORGA.

5.1.2 Tooling Office engineer shall be responsible for setting up a tooling acceptance team by inviting Program Management, Technical Office of Science Technology &Informatization, Quality Control Office and tooling using department, to conduct unpacking inspection. If necessary, customer representative shall also be invited.

5.1.3 Inspection personnel of the unit using the tooling shall fill in Form ANF8627-36 according to Packing Sheet and actual packing condition. Meanwhile, personnel participating in tooling acceptance shall sign on Form ANF8627-36.

5.1.4 Tooling Office ofTechnological Innovation Department t shall print and distribute customer-supplied tooling drawings. Official tooling drawings shall be kept by tooling user.

5.1.5 The workshop planer will include the customer tooling into the tooling variety table, and the technician in the process equipment room will include the customer tooling into the tooling catalog for management.

5.2  Storage and Borrowing of Master Tooling

5.2.1  Master tooling (including documentation materials) shall be kept and controlled by tooling using department, and ANF 4172-46 shall be established.

5.2.2  In case that master tooling is borrowed and returned, inspector, borrower and tooling keeper shall confirm condition of master tooling together. Master tooling keeper shall be responsible for confirming the quantity of borrowed and returned master gauge and fill in block “Condition” in Form ANF 4172-46. Inspector shall be responsible for checking appearance quality of master tooling and affix stamp in block “Inspector” of Form ANF 4172-46 if the appearance quality is acceptable. And borrower and keeper shall apply signature or stamp in Form ANF 4172-46.

5.2.3 Master tooling shall be borrowed together with its packing box and shall be kept in the box timely after use.

5.2.4  Inspector shall be responsible for lead sealing and unsealing. Master tooling stored in storage shall be under condition of lead sealing.

5.3  Storage and Borrowing of Production and Inspection Tooling

5.3.1 Production and inspection tooling (including documentation materials) shall be kept by user, and Form ANF7316-15 shall be established.

5.3.2  Production and inspection tooling shall be stored in tooling storage or on production site, per the contour dimension. Tooling and tooling log book shall match up with each other.

5.3.3  Production and inspection tooling shall be borrowed and returned, borrow person and keeper sign the name on ANF7316-15.

5.3.4 The package of the produce and check Tooling shall be kept well and turn away lost and demolished.

5.4  Tooling Maintenance

Tooling Maintenanceaccording to company proceduremanagement Procedure for Tooling Usage

5.5  Use of Tooling

5.5.1  Use ofCustomer-Supplied Toolingaccording to company proceduremanagement.

5.5.2  Production tooling or inspection tooling in the process of use, damage or problems affect the use, should be reported to the technical director of the unit in time, the technical director to inform the process equipment room, the process equipment room technician to organize relevant departments to the site for analysis and treatment, and report to the project management department, if necessary by the project management manager to inform the customer. At the same time, notice of suspension and detention of tooling (ANF 4841-47).

5.5.3 When the standard tooling is damaged or affected by problems during use, the user of the standard tooling shall notify the technician in the process equipment room, and the technician in the process equipment room shall notify the technical office, and the technical Office shall organize relevant departments to analyze and deal with the problem on site. The inspection department shall fill in the unqualified tooling review form (ANF 1678-43) and report it to the project manager of the project management Department, who shall inform the customer if necessary. At the same time, notice of suspension and detention of tooling (ANF 4841-47).

5.5.4  Epoxy is forbidden to directly contact with customer-supplied master tooling.

5.5.5 After the customer tooling is received, before the first one is put into production, it shall be verified in accordance with the requirements ofDOPB83343573. After the verification, it can be put into formal production.

5.6 Transportation and Lifting of Tooling

Master tooling shall be borrowed together with its packing box. Specific person shall be assigned for its transportation by the vehicle. The vehicle can only move after safety is checked and confirmed. If tooling needs to be lifted, lifting points shall be checked carefully to assure safety. When customers have specific requirements for lifting, they shall provide standard lifting requirements.

5.7  Periodic Inspection of Tooling

According to the contract, if Shenyang Civil Aircraft Corporation orders the tooling provided by the customer, the planner of the tooling using department shall propose the inspection requirements according to the inspection plan, and the technician of process equipment room shall organize the tooling inspection; For the tooling to be inspected by the customer, the workshop planner will put forward the inspection demand one month in advance, and the process equipment room will submit it to the project management department, where the project manager will coordinate with the customer to carry out the inspection work.

5.8  Modification of Tooling

5.8.1 Without customer authority, no change is allowed to make on customer-supplied tooling.

5.8.2 The tooling provided by the customer shall not be altered, repaired or reworked without the authorization of the customer.

5.8.3 Within 15 working days after the user receives the tooling from the customer, the technician shall include the tooling from the customer into the tooling variety table for unified management and indicate the tooling from the customer in the remarks.

5.9  Customer tooling returned to customer

5.9.1 When returning the tooling as required by the customer, the project manager of the project management department shall inform the technician in the process equipment Room of the time and place of tooling return provided by the customer.

5.9.2 The process equipment room shall organize the user to open the box to check the physical and document status of the tooling, notify the project and invite the designated personnel of the customer to the site for verification, and after the verification is completed, the inspection technician of the user unit shall take photos of the physical tooling and seal the packing box for shipment.

5.9.3 Project Management Department The project manager fills out the packing list according to the requirements of customers and sends customers for tooling.

5.9.4 The customer tooling due to the project shutdown is handled by the project manager of the project management Department after consultation with the customer, forming a memorandum and handling according to the customer's requirements.
