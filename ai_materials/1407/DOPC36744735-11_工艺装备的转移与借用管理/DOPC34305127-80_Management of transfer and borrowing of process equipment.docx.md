Management standard for the transfer and borrowing of process equipment

1  Scope

This procedure stipulates the management measures for the transfer and borrowing of process equipment.

This procedure is applicable to both military and civil aircraft projects.

4  Terms and definitions

 

4.1 Tooling transfer

 The activity of transferring a complete set of assets and documents, such as physical tooling, certificates of conformity, drawings, and digital models, from the unit before the division of labor change to the unit after the change, due to a change in the division of labor route.

4.2 Borrowing of tooling

 An activity where the borrowing unit temporarily borrows tooling from the lending unit due to their temporary needs, only temporarily changing the responsibility for tooling use and maintenance, without changing the responsibility for tooling asset management.

5  Regulations

5.1 The Process Equipment Room of the Science and Technology Innovation Department is the supervisory unit responsible for formulating the process of tooling transfer and borrowing. Responsible for organizing various units to carry out tooling transfer activities according to the tooling transfer plan. Responsible for approving tooling borrowing applications. The Tooling Design Office of the Science and Technology Innovation Department is responsible for the transfer and reception of tooling models, drawings, and datasets.

5.2 The tooling user units (including Shenyang Aircraft Corporation, outsourced parts suppliers, and various production plants within the company) are responsible for the preparation of tooling transfer materials, physical preparation, and physical acceptance. Responsible for proposing and approving the borrowing of tooling.

5.3 The project management department is responsible for issuing work transfer initiation instructions and co signing the tooling transfer plan.

5.4 The Supply Chain Management Department is responsible for providing notification of changes in business division of labor to the Process Equipment Department of the Science and Technology Innovation Department.

5.5 The Production and Operations Department is responsible for preparing a tooling transfer plan according to production needs.

5.2 Tool Transfer

5.2.1 The initiation of tooling transfer work shall be based on the approved ANF3357-06 "Work Transfer Application Form". The tooling technician in the Process Equipment Room of the Science and Technology Innovation Department prepares the ANF3786-35 "Tooling Transfer Plan" based on the nodes specified in the ANF3357-06 "Work Transfer Application Form".

5.2.2 The tooling transfer unit shall identify the list of transferred tooling according to the division of labor instructions and provide the tooling with transfer conditions.

5.2.2.1 The unit transferring the tooling shall submit an ANF after receiving the tooling transfer plan 1405-47 "List of Process Equipment Varieties".

5.2.2.2 The unit transferring the tooling shall submit the signed factory layout plan to the Ministry of Science and Technology Innovation within the specified time of the tooling transfer plan. The co signing of the tooling transfer plan is divided into two stages. The first stage is to transfer the schedule. The second stage is to transfer the signed record column for the handover of physical tooling, tooling drawings and models, and tooling qualification certificates within the transfer plan.

5.2.3 The tooling transfer out unit and the tooling transfer in unit shall carry out the transfer work according to the transfer plan timeline.

5.2.3.1 The tooling transfer unit is responsible for preparing the physical tooling, tooling drawings and models, tooling qualification certificates, and tooling acceptance materials.

5.2.3.2 The tooling transfer unit shall dispatch process technicians and inspectors to receive and verify the physical quantity, surface quality, tooling acceptance data, tooling drawing numerical models, and tooling qualification certificates of the tooling.

5.2.3.3 After confirming the correctness of the transfer of tooling to and from the unit, both parties shall sign and confirm in the relevant column of the tooling transfer plan.

During the process of transferring tooling, any missing, damaged, or corroded tooling parts must be repaired by the tooling transfer unit before they can be transferred out.

5.2.3.5 The confirmation column of the tooling transfer plan has been signed, and any missing, damaged, or corroded tooling parts found will be transferred to the unit for repair.When the receiving unit is a production unit within the company, the vehicle used in the tooling transfer process shall be requested for use by the tooling transfer unit.

5.2.3.7 When the receiving unit is Shenyang Aircraft Corporation or a parts supplier, the vehicle used in the tooling transfer process shall be requested for use by the process equipment room.

5.2.3.8 If a vehicle application needs to be submitted by the Process Equipment Room, the Supply Chain Management Department shall provide the budget requirements to the Process Equipment Room according to the business division of labor when submitting the annual budget report.After the transfer of tooling is completed, the signed version of the tooling transfer plan shall be retained by the archives room of the Technical Office of the Ministry of Science and Technology Innovation.

5.2.6  ERP data changes after tooling transfer is completed.

5.2.6.1 Shenyang Aircraft Corporation's civil aircraft assets shall be transferred to the ERP system by the tooling transfer unit.

 5.2.6.2 Non Shenyang Aircraft assets shall be created with ERP data by the tooling receiving unit.

5.3 Borrowing of Tooling

5.3.1 When borrowing tooling, the borrowing unit shall submit the ANF0783-81 "Tooling Borrowing Application Form". Two copies of the application form shall be retained by the tooling planners of the borrowing and lending units respectively.

5.3.2 The planner of the tooling borrowing unit shall conduct ANF at the tooling lending unit Sign on the 1405-27 'Tooling Management Card'.

5.3.3 The borrowing of tooling is limited to Class II and Class III tooling, and the borrowing of Class I tooling is prohibited.

5.3.4 The borrowing of tooling is limited to the physical borrowing of tooling, and the tooling qualification certificate and acceptance materials shall be kept by the tooling lending unit.

5.3.5 In the process of borrowing tooling, any damage, missing parts, or loss of tooling shall be repaired and made up by the borrowing unit.

5.3.6 If the process of borrowing tooling involves inventory of tooling assets, the tooling lending unit shall be responsible for conducting an inventory of the borrowed tooling. The borrowing unit of tooling needs to cooperate with the lending unit of tooling to conduct asset inventory.

5.3.7 The borrowing unit of the tooling shall return the tooling within the time limit specified in the borrowing application form and sign on the ANF 3882-43 "Tooling Management Card".

5.3.8 During the borrowing period of the tooling, the borrowing unit shall complete the daily maintenance and upkeep of the tooling in accordance with DOPB61644334 "Process Equipment Usage Management".
