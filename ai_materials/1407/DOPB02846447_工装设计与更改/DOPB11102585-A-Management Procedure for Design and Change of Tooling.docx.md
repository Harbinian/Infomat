Management Procedure for Design and Change of Tooling

1 Scope

This management procedure specifies methods for design, change, review and release of the tooling drawings and methods for creating and management of tooling usage instruction, management of tooling data created by suppliers.

This management procedure is applicable to commercial aircraft production.

This management procedure is developed according to the requirements of DOPA3813.4 Normative Reference Files

4 Terms and Definitions

4.1 Tooling

It refers to special tools and equipment used to process and manufacture aircraft parts and components, ensure aircraft assembly accuracy, coordinate aircraft structure and system installation accuracy, test the functional characteristics of various aircraft systems, and ensure the safe, reliable and stable flight test of aircraft. It mainly includes standard process equipment, production process equipment, special tools and templates. This management procedure tooling design includes product packaging design, tooling design, specialized tool design and sample manufacturing dataset design.

4.2 Self-made tooling

It refers to the tooling, special tools, templates, etc. that are designed, manufactured and accepted by the tooling user according to the process specification.

4.3 Tooling Revision

For the Boeing program, if the customer requires the new tooling to be controlled according to the tooling drawing template and tooling revision, the implementation of revision upgrade principle is - A, B, C. The other tooling and template revision upgrade principle is T1, T2, and T3.

4.4 Translation and Duplication

Tooling drawings provided by customers shall be translated and dimensions called out shall be converted.

4.5 Maturity of tooling data

In the process of tooling design, the completion degree of tooling data that reaches certain technical status and requirements.

4.6 Antecedence Change

In specifically conditions and efficiency period, temporarily dispose for tooling in design, manufacture or finding partial problem in using .It is changed before tooling data change edition and perform edition control and management.

4.7 Tooling Design Review

The process of scheme demonstration and review for the structure, manufacturing process, operability, functionality, reliability, economy and safety of tooling design.

4.8 Important tooling

Important tooling refers to the major assembly tooling, test equipment which has big influence on production line or safety, or the tooling which with particularity or big controversial in process method/structure/performance index.

4.9 Engineering dataset

Engineering dataset is a set of data, which is a result designed by the engineering design department, containing the information such as geometry, structure, processing, etc. of parts, assembly parts, installation parts in one or several CAD models, according to certain logic structure.

4.10 Tooling Data

Tooling Data is a collection of data, is the design result of Tooling Design Department. It includes geometry, structure and processing information of detail parts and assemblies into one or several CAD models according to certain logical structure. It includes tooling drawing in paper and the 3D/2D tooling dataset generated from CAD system.4.11 Manufacturing dataset

Manufacturing dataset is a set of data, which is a result designed by the engineering manufacturing department. It is based on engineering dataset with a variety of process manufacturing information added according to process requirements. It is a model with integrated product information and can be directly used for tooling design and manufacturing, part processing.

4.12 Process digital model

Tooling Dataset is a collection of data, is the design result of Tooling Design Department. It includes geometry, structure and processing information of detail parts and assemblies into one or several CAD models according to certain logical structure. It includes tooling drawing in paper and the 3D/2D tooling data generated from CAD system.

4.13 Tooling Dataset

Tooling Dataset is a collection of data, is the design result of Tooling Design Department. It includes geometry, structure and processing information of detail parts and assemblies into one or several CAD models according to certain logical structure. It includes tooling drawing in paper and the 3D/2D tooling data generated from CAD system.

4.14 Tooling Drawing

The paper medium type expression information and expression ways of tooling data include vitriol paper type, blue print type, white print type, film type and so on.

4.15 2D Tooling Data

2D Tooling Data is two-dimensional plane description formed by CAD system two-dimensional function module or by 3D tooling dada projecting.

4.16 3D Tooling Data

3D tooling data is three-dimensional visual tooling digital model and relevant tooling data information formed by CAD system.

5 Responsibilities

5.1 Tooling using unit

The tooling using unit is responsible for applying for tooling, Template and Product Packaging. The planer is responsible for providing design materials internally or externally, reviewing and signing the tooling data, and checking whether the data meets the use requirements and safety requirements. The planner of tooling user shop is responsible for creating Tooling Usage Instruction expect for test equipment. The planer is responsible for designing Self-made tooling.

5.2 Department of Technological Innovation

The Department of Technological Innovation is responsible for Designing and Changing of Tooling data, organize tooling design review, outsourced technical support, signing and receiving data. The Department of Technological Innovation distribute the data and keep on a file. Department of Technological Innovation is responsible for proposing outsourcing applications for design and manufacturing, controlling the design and manufacturing progress of tooling and samples, provides data to suppliers, and acceptance, creating operating instruction for test equipment.

5.3 Tooling Manufacturer

Tooling Manufacturer is responsible for Manufacture Tooling refer to the data. The planner of tooling manufacturing unit is responsible for reviewing whether the processing technology of the tooling design is reasonable.

6.3 Requirement for tooling design and modification

6.3.1Reference of the tooling design and modification

Reference of the tooling design and modification includes Tooling Production Plan, Tooling Application Form, Engineering drawings and Technical Requirements, Engineering Data and Lofting Templates.

6.3.2 Requirements of Tooling Design

6.3.2.1 Tooling design must meet all technical requirements with reasonable structure, sufficient rigidity and strength, safe and reliable positioning and operation. For the Boeing program, supplier should comply with the requirements of D33200 (-1,-2, BOEING SUPPLIER'S TOOLING) document and continuously meet the latest version of the document.

6.3.2.2 Tooling design should consider error-proofing, prevent the using error.

6.3.2.3 Materials and purchased products shall be economically and reasonably selected.

6.3.2.4 Properly select manufacturing references to ensure the coordination and interchangeability requirements of the product.

6.3.2.5 The manufacturing process should be good and easy for fabrication.

6.3.2.6 Choose all kinds of standard parts and finished products as far as possible.

6.3.2.7 Actively adopt reliable new technology, new technology and new structure.

6.3.2.8 National mechanical drafting standard and technical standard, enterprise technical standard currently implemented in the company and customers’ special requirements shall be followed.

6.3.2.9 The selection of the datum needs to reach the unity of process datum and design datum and ensure the precision of the products and the accuracy of assembly when datum conversion.

6.3.2.10 For the production tooling that need to use the master gauge (measure gauge, sample part etc.) as the coordinating criterion, mark the coordination criterion of this tooling on the tooling pattern.

6.3.2.11 For type I and type II tooling, mark the necessary datum line, datum plane, tooling ball number or measuring points so as to perform the tooling manufacture and periodical inspection.

6.3.2.12 Tooling design should stipulate the requirement of tooling identification and surface decoration etc.

6.3.2.13 When using CATIA V5 software for 3D modeling of tooling design, the general environment settings, relevant regulations of coordinate system, requirements for the proportion, size and unit of part digital modeling, basic principles of modeling and general requirements for modeling, and requirements for naming data sets shall be in accordance with the current industry standards and the company's enterprise standards.

6.3.2.14 Tooling design drawings of Boeing project shall be attached with the list of removable parts.

6.3.2.15 The tooling design of Type I and II of Boeing Program shall be in English and Chinese, and with metric and Britain system.

6.3.3 Requirements for Changes to Tooling Drawings

6.3.3.1 Reasons for Changes

a）Changes of the engineering data and technical documents.

b）Change of the Planning Sheet and machine tools/equipment.

c）Improvement of the manufacturability and efficiency.

d）Improvement of the design due to problems discovered during trial use of the tooling.

e）Design error.

6.3.3.2 Change Principles

6.3.3.2.1 Take ANF2172-58 to record and management tooling drawing revision.

6.3.3.2.2 The version of ANF2172-58 corresponds one to one with the version of the tooling drawing homepage. The revision of the form starts from B01, and is upgraded according to B02, B03, etc. The tooling drawing homepage version starts from T1 and is upgraded according to T1, T2, etc.

6.3.3.2.3 If the tooling has been reviewed and approved by the customer, any changes shall be subject to the customer’s review and approval.

6.3.3.2.4 When copying tooling drawing, if obvious errors are found in the original drawing, the designer is permitted to correct them, with the exception of standard tooling. When making such corrections, the revision level shall not be advanced. Instead, the suffixes A, B, etc., shall be added to the original revision. The changes must be communicated to the customer. However, errors in critical dimensions must be confirmed by the customer prior to any modification.

6.3.3.3 Change Method

6.3.3.3.1 Tooling drawing Antecedence Change take ANF6867-51 perform management and explaining, it can be divided into G and J (with two-dimensional tooling drawing).The routing for duplication and release of Antecedence Change is the same as tooling drawing, and shall be kept with tooling drawing together as the basis for the inspection and acceptance of the tooling. ANF6867-51 must uniquely correspond to the number of pages that have been changed in advance with 2D tooling data. If class G and class J changes occur successively on the same page of tooling drawing, they are sorted according to the number of self-change times of their type of change. For example：G01, G02, J01, J02.

6.3.3.3.2 When five copies of ANF4465-66 are released, the drawing upgrade should be carried out, and the change of tooling drawing should be managed and explained by ANF3304-32.

6.3.3.3.3 Before new design or customer approval, the version of pre released tooling data shall be sorted by the maturity level of tooling data Y50, Y70, etc., and the version of management sheet shall be sorted by YB01, YB02, etc.

6.3.3.3.4 Each time a revision level is updated (no matter whether the general drawing is changed or not), the revision level of the general drawing shall be updated accordingly. In order to maintain good quality of the tooling general drawing, large-sized general drawing shall be divided into two or more sheets, the first sheet shall carry the detailed list, revision change record and the record of revisions of each sheet while the other sheet shall be drawings. Therefore, the Revision No. in the title column on the first sheet of the tooling general drawing shall be the revision No. of the tooling, and this shall be only the updated revision, i.e. only T1, T2… without footnote letters.

6.3.3.3.5 If the tooling design change and the tooling change have any potential impact on the physical/functional characteristics (fit, contour or function) of the part, assembly/installation and/or the next higher level assembly/installation, the revision level shall be updated to the next level, i.e. T2, T3, and so on.

6.3.3.3.6 ANF3304-32 should be filled in when the tooling drawing is changed, which is used to change the vellum. The route for release of the Change Note for the vellum shall be same as that for release of the drawing.

6.3.3.3.7 Tooling blueprints shall be changed with ink or pen with legible handwriting. The figures or numbers should be lined out but not altered or scratched off. A change mark should be made at the changed places， designer signs and date the drawings.

6.3.3.3.8 Change mark shall be a fine solid circle of φ8, in which the change number shall be written, for example: change mark T1A shall be T eq \o\ac(○,1A), and change mark for T2 shall be T eq \o\ac(○,2).

6.3.3.3.9 If a drawing with correct design has to be changed temporarily due to some reason, the change shall be made by the designer who must prepare ANF4465-66 to describe the change contents for approval.

6.3.3.3.10 If the tooling design and tooling are reviewed and approved by the customer, above-stated changes must be subject to review and approval by the customer。6.3.3.3.11 When the tooling changes significantly and cannot be repaired but can only be remade, it is allowed to change the overall version of the tooling drawings.

6.4 Requirement for Tooling design review

6.4.1 Tooling design review (excluding class I and class II tooling of Boeing program) is divided into three stages: concept design review (PDR), critical design review (CDR) and final design review (FDR). Class I and class II tooling design review of Boeing program is divided into five stages: initial conceptual design review stage (new made), functional design review stage (PDR), detail design review stage (CDR), manufacturing preparation design review stage (FDR) and formal release stage.

6.4.2 Scope of Tooling Design Review

Scope of Tooling require design review:

a）Important tooling.

b）Tooling using new material, new technical.

c）Tooling with uncertainty in process method, design scheme.

d）Class I and class II tooling of Boeing program.

e）Tooling controlled by ORGB, and assembly tooling of component.

6.4.3 Basis of Tooling Design Review

The review basis of tooling design includes: tooling budget, general process plan, assembly coordination plan, tooling variety table, tooling application form, engineering drawing, engineering data, manufacturing data set, loft template, standard tooling, etc.

6.4.4 Review Team Members

Review Team Leader: supervisor of Tooling Design Office

Team members: tooling designers, planners and other relevant personnel (program VP if necessary), involving large and important assembly tooling, ground equipment and electrical production line, Safety Management Office personnel are invited to participate.

6.4.5 Problem Solving and Follow up

The review team leader shall sort out the problems raised in the review and the rectification plan, urge the designers to carry out the rectification, form the action program, assign the responsibility to the person and specify the deadline.

6.4.6 Preservation of Tooling Review Minutes

The review minutes are stored in electronic form in the distribution area, and create a folder according to program for query.

6.5 Requirement for distribution of tooling data

6.5.1 Create tooling data

6.5.1.1 Packing Dataset is established according to DOPC42144755-53.

6.5.1.2 Manufacture Dataset is established according to DOPC16881252-51.

6.5.1.3 Tooling Dataset is established according to DOPC37378152-55.

6.5.2 Tooling Data Release

6.5.2.1 The formal release of tooling data must have the process of examining and signing, and any tooling data without examining and signing process cannot be released. The latest edition of tooling data is effective.

6.5.2.2 Tooling data is electronic, and issued paper media drawing on demand.

6.5.2.3 After the process of examining and signing, the documenter of Technical office release the data according to the distribution route, and stored in the distribution area or dedicated computer.

6.6 Requirement for Tooling requires Operating instruction

6.6.1 Tooling requires Operating instruction shall be created for the following tooling: class II process equipment, inspecting jig, finishing jig, test equipment, tooling with detachable parts, tooling of Boeing program.

6.6.2 Distribution of Tooling Usage Instruction: 2 for user department, 1 for archives center, 1 for operator hanging on the tooling, original copy is stored in creator's office.

6.6.3 Serial number of Tooling Usage Instruction

6.6.4 Content of Tooling Usage Instruction

6.6.4.1 Matters need attention.

6.6.4.2 Assistant tooling to be used together.

6.6.4.3 Operating sequence.

6.6.4.4 When the tooling contains detachable parts, the position and fixing method of the detachable parts after disassembly, as well as the error prevention measures for reinstallation, should be explained in the usage instruction.

6.6.5 Requirements for reviewing training and filing of Tooling Usage Instruction

6.6.5.1 After the preparation of the Tooling Usage Instruction, organize relevant technical personnel, inspectors, operators, etc. for review by the preparation unit. After approval, the preparation unit sends it to the technical unit sends it to the technical office data officer to upload it to the Lantai file management system for backup.

6.6.5.2 After the preparation or upgrade of the Tooling Usage Instruction, the preparation or manual, the preparer shall provide training to the tooling users and inspectors and keep training records.

6.6.6.3 When the tooling is circulated between different locations, the Tooling Usage Instruction author needs to pass it on to all users.

6.7 Requirement for Outsourced Tooling Design Data

6.7.1 Provision of outsourced design materials

6.7.1.1 The process engineer of the tooling user unit shall provide the following outsourced design materials to the supplier according to the outsourced tooling project:

a) Process Equipment Application Form (Technical Conditions for Tooling Design);

b) Product data (engineering dataset, process specifications, manufacturing dataset, process separation surface, handover status, etc.);

c) Other data related to the design or modification of fixtures, samples, etc.

6.7.1.2 The technician of Tooling Office is responsible for proposing outsourcing applications for design and manufacturing, controlling the design and manufacturing progress of tooling and samples, provides data to suppliers, and acceptance, creating operating instruction for test equipment.

6.7.2 Outsourcing Design Review

6.7.2.1 Conduct design review on important fixtures, fixtures that apply new materials and technologies, fixtures with uncertain process plans or design plans, and fixtures that require review according to customer documents for each project. The supervisor of the tooling design office shall organize relevant personnel to conduct design review according to section 6.2 procedure.

6.7.2.2 For fixtures that fail the design review, they shall be marked as unqualified on ANF7434-65, and the supplier shall redesign and reevaluate them.

6.7.2.3 After completion of external tooling design, tooling Design experts organized by them to conduct tooling design data review signatures according to Section 6.3.2.

6.7.3 Technical Tracking of Outsourced Design

6.5.3.1 The designer of the tooling design room is responsible for answering technical questions raised by suppliers during the outsourcing design and manufacturing process.

6.7.3.2 The technician in the process equipment room is responsible for tracking changes in the version of the process equipment application form and organizing the tooling user unit to provide the modified outsourced design data to the supplier.

6.7.4 Outsourced Design Changes

6.7.4.1 After the outsourcing tooling data is reviewed and signed, if there are changes caused by the upgrade of the process equipment application form, the supplier needs to fill out the tooling data change form ANF3304-32 and complete the changes in the tooling data.

6.7.4.2 The outsourced tooling data change form reviewed and signed by the tooling design room can serve as the basis for accepting physical tooling.

6.7.4.3 Any changes that occur before the receipt of outsourced tooling data shall be the responsibility of the supplier, and any changes that occur after the receipt of outsourced tooling data shall be the responsibility of the tooling design studio designer.
