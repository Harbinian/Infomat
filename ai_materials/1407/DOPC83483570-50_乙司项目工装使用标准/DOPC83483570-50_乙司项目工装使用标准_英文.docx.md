Tooling Usage Management Standard for ORGB Projects

1 Scope

This document specifies the requirements for the management and maintenance of ORGB tooling.This document applies to the implementation of tooling business for ORGB projects.This document is formulated in accordance with DOPB06132724 and SR0006.

4 Terms and Definitions

4.1 Supplier Tooling List

A document containing tooling information (including tooling serial number, tooling drawing number, tooling name, etc.) for all tooling (including decommissioned, scrapped, retained, etc.).

4.2 Qualified Supplier Tooling List

A document containing tooling information (including tooling serial number, tooling drawing number, tooling name, etc.) for tooling that has passed inspection and is used for normal production.

4.3 ORGB Controlled Tooling Project List

A document selected by ORGB from the Supplier Tooling List based on relevant tooling control principles, containing tooling information (including tooling serial number, tooling drawing number, tooling name, etc.) for tooling that requires ORGB control.

4.4 Supplier Tooling Asset List

A document containing asset information (ownership belongs to ORGB) of tooling that has been manufactured and warehoused, including tooling serial number, tooling drawing number, tooling name, etc.

4.5 Qualified Tooling

Tooling that meets the usage requirements.

5 Provisions

5.1 Tooling Project Approval

5.1.1 Tooling Numbering

Unless otherwise specified, the numbering of tooling drawings shall be implemented in accordance with the requirements of Procedure DOPC8676103203. However, the corresponding tooling code (implemented in accordance with Q/CTS 0030) shall be listed in the "Supplier Tooling List" (see Appendix B).The tooling serial number shall be implemented in accordance with Appendix E.

5.1.2 Tooling Selection

5.1.2.1 Determination of ORGB Controlled Tooling Projects

(1) Determination Principles:a) Type I and Type II tooling (excluding those provided by ORGB);b) Part inspection tooling for key parts/important parts;c) Other tooling that ORGB deems necessary to control.

(2) Within ten working days after the Project Management Department submits the component assembly coordination plan to ORGB and obtains approval, it shall submit the electronic version (EXCEL format) and paper version (signed by the project manager) of the Supplier Tooling List (Appendix B) required for the manufactured components to ORGB;

(3) The Project Management Department shall organize ORGB to select ORGB controlled tooling projects from the provided Supplier Tooling List (Appendix B) and compile the electronic version (PDF format) of the ORGB Controlled Tooling Project List (Appendix A);

(4) If there is a change to the tooling project, the Process Equipment Office shall update the Supplier Tooling List. The Project Management Department shall submit the updated Supplier Tooling List (Appendix B, with explanations in the remarks column, such as: new addition, duplication, deletion, controlled, etc.) to ORGB within ten working days after confirming the change. The Project Management Department shall coordinate with ORGB to decide whether to update the ORGB Controlled Tooling Project List (Appendix A) based on the content of the change.

5.1.2.2 The Process Equipment Office shall establish and maintain the "Qualified Supplier Tooling List" for each project.

5.1.2.3 Within one month after the delivery of the first component, the Project Management Department shall provide the updated Supplier Tooling List (Appendix B) (as of the component delivery date) to ORGB. At the same time, it shall be updated in a timely manner in accordance with configuration control requirements until the delivery of ORGB's first aircraft, and organize ORGB to compile it into the project tooling list.

5.2 Tooling Management

5.2.1 Tooling Asset Management

5.2.1.1 At the end of each year, summarize the information of tooling completed and warehoused in the current year into the "Supplier Tooling Asset List" (Appendix C), and submit the electronic version (EXCEL format) and paper version (signed by the project manager) to ORGB.

5.2.1.2 The Project Management Department is responsible for handling ORGB's regular (first half of each year) or irregular tooling asset inspection and inventory work for tooling assets (ownership belongs to ORGB).

5.2.1.3 After the Process Equipment Office organizes the completion of the tooling asset inspection and inventory work, the Project Management Department and ORGB shall sign and confirm the results. If there are shortages, surpluses, or other situations during the tooling asset inspection and inventory process, the Project Management Department shall formulate rectification measures and notify ORGB in writing of the implementation status, which shall be verified by ORGB.

5.2.2 Management and Maintenance

5.2.2.1 Unless otherwise specified, the Process Equipment Office shall implement the requirements of Procedure DOPB61644334 to ensure effective control of tooling management and maintenance.

5.2.2.2 The management requirements for standard tooling shall be implemented in accordance with DOPC68606338-02.

5.2.2.3 The tooling user unit shall conduct daily maintenance work such as visual inspection, rust removal, oiling, and cleaning of inter-factory shared tooling in accordance with the requirements of Procedure DOPB61644334 and form records. If any faults or damages are found during the use of inter-factory shared tooling, the specific situation shall be reported to the Process Equipment Office in a timely manner. After verification and confirmation by the Process Equipment Office, it shall be reported to the Domestic Project Office, which shall then report to ORGB.

5.2.2.4 The regular inspection of inter-factory shared tooling shall be coordinated by the Project Management Department and undertaken by ORGB, or entrusted to the Process Equipment Office for regular inspection (when the tooling is at AVIC Shenyang Aircraft Industry (Group) Civil Aircraft) in accordance with DOPB158513. The Project Management Department shall coordinate with ORGB's quality representative to participate in the acceptance. For tooling subject to periodic inspection by "aircraft unit", the Department of Science and Technology Innovation shall cooperate with ORGB (quality representative) to complete the relevant regular inspection procedures and information filling, that is, fill in the specific aircraft unit information used by the tooling in the Periodic Inspection (SAMC 05-50, Appendix D).

5.2.2.5 The tooling user unit shall conduct daily work such as visual inspection, rust removal, oiling, and cleaning of all tooling on-site or in the warehouse and form records. If problems such as missing tooling components, rust, poor lubrication, missing nameplates, damaged nameplates, damaged electrical circuits, hydraulic/pneumatic leakage, and potential safety hazards are found, they shall be reported to the Process Equipment Office for handling in a timely manner.

5.2.2.6 In addition to daily maintenance work, the tooling user unit shall perform tooling maintenance in accordance with the specific requirements of the tooling drawings, including ensuring the completeness of tooling components, good usable condition, clear nameplates/identification, clean surface, no obvious appearance defects, no visually visible potential safety hazards, smoothness inspection of sliding parts, repair and replacement of special components, and elimination of potential safety hazards.

5.2.2.7 During the tooling acceptance process, the tooling manufacturing unit shall mark the tooling serial number in the delivery documents, and the user unit shall check this requirement during the acceptance process.

5.2.2.8 ORGB has the right to assign representatives to inspect any relevant tooling and related data of the supplier.

5.2.3 ORGB Controlled Tooling Project List

The Process Equipment Office shall establish and maintain the ORGB Controlled Tooling Project List, which shall include at least the following information: tooling drawing number (tooling category code, sequence number), copy number, name, regular inspection cycle, next regular inspection date, etc.

5.2.4 Tooling Duplication, Scrapping and Decommissioning

5.2.4.1 Duplicated Tooling

(1) Duplication: Existing tooling projects may be applied for duplication to expand production capacity, but different tooling serial numbers and copy numbers shall be assigned for distinction. After the completion of manufacturing, the tooling can be put into use only after passing inspection by quality personnel (including production verification);(2) Duplication of Scrapped Tooling: Tooling that cannot be repaired due to normal wear and tear or modifications shall be scrapped and duplicated. All scrapped and duplicated tooling shall retain the original tooling number/copy number and use a new tooling serial number. After the completion of manufacturing, the tooling can be put into use only after passing inspection by quality personnel (including production verification).

5.2.4.2 Scrapped Tooling

The scrapping process of tooling used for ORGB projects shall be implemented in accordance with DOPB17023245. The special requirements involving ORGB are as follows:The approval for the scrapping of tooling (ownership belongs to ORGB) shall be implemented in accordance with the following requirements:(1) For the scrapping of tooling projects owned by ORGB, the tooling asset unit shall submit a written application with physical photos of the tooling to be scrapped as an attachment (confirmed by the chief engineer) to ORGB for approval;(2) Without written authorization from ORGB, the Financial Management Department shall not dispose of the residual value of the tooling.

5.2.4.3 Decommissioning of ORGB Controlled Tooling Projects

The decommissioning of tooling used for ORGB projects shall be implemented in accordance with the relevant processes for tooling sealing specified in DOPB61644334. The specific requirements involving ORGB are as follows:(1) If tooling is decommissioned due to process changes, it shall be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the internal procedures, but the change shall be reported to ORGB for the record;(2) If tooling is decommissioned due to engineering configuration changes, the Project Management Department may submit a written application to ORGB, and ORGB shall issue handling opinions on the tooling.

5.3 ORGB Inter-factory Shared Tooling

5.3.1 Design

The Project Management Department shall coordinate with ORGB to be responsible for the design and change control of all inter-factory shared tooling.

5.3.2 Manufacturing

5.3.2.1 Inter-factory shared tooling shall be manufactured by ORGB coordinated by the Project Management Department in accordance with ORGB's tooling drawings/data, or may be entrusted to suppliers for manufacturing, but shall be included in ORGB's tooling control system.

5.3.2.2 The identification and surface coating of inter-factory shared tooling shall be implemented in accordance with the requirements of Q/CTS 0039 and Q/C TS 0040 respectively.

5.3.3 Loan and Return

5.3.3.1 Tooling borrowed with products (or separately) shall be accompanied by complete tooling identification, tooling quality documents, tooling packing list, and other data. The shipping party shall retain verifiable documents for the tooling.

5.3.3.2 The shipping/receiving party shall complete the written handover procedures for the tooling in accordance with the tooling certification data, etc.

5.3.3.3 Before using the inter-factory tooling and before returning it for shipment (including shipment with products), a status inspection shall be conducted (such as: regular inspection cycle, consistency between the physical version of the tooling and the latest effective tooling version required by SPS, etc.), and any problematic items (including those found during use) shall be reported in writing to the on-site quality representative. Subsequent work shall not be continued until the disposal opinions on the problematic items are obtained from ORGB's Procurement and Supply Department. Before using the inter-factory tooling, the tooling drawing number, name, version, and its consistency with the latest effective tooling version required by SPS shall be recorded in the assembly process documents/manufacturing process documents. The inter-factory tooling shall be returned to ORGB for regular inspection three months before the expiration of the regular inspection cycle, unless it is in use.
