Management Procedure for Tooling Verification

1 Scope

This document stipulates the process of verifying the capability of tooling to meet product manufacturing or assembly requirements after the acceptance of process equipment and before its use in production, as well as the process of collecting statistical data on tooling performance after it is put into production

This document is formulated in accordance with DOPA7805 Tooling Management Rules.

5 Institutions and Responsibilities

5.1 Science and Technology Innovation Department

The Process and Equipment Office of the Science and Technology Innovation Department is responsible for organizing the tooling manufacturing units to rework the problems found during the tooling verification process;

5.2 Tooling usage unit

The unit using the tooling is responsible for verifying the tooling used by itself.

6.3 Requirements for Fixture Verification Management

6.3.1 The fixtures that need to be verified include: Class II and III newly made fixtures that form, control, and inspect the geometric features of the product (hereinafter referred to as Class II and III fixtures refer to the fixtures that need to be verified).

6.3.2 Tooling validation should be conducted from the first production batch/shipment after the acceptance of the new tooling, to verify whether the corresponding product characteristics controlled by the tooling are qualified. Three sets of Class II tooling need to be validated to confirm its stability, and one set of Class III tooling needs to be validated.

6.3.3 The tooling inspection personnel of the tooling user unit shall fill out the ANF5032-23 tooling verification checklist after the acceptance of the new tooling and before the tooling is put into use, and transmit the ANF5032-23 to the process personnel to fill in the product information for the planned tooling verification. The process personnel shall transmit the filled ANF5032-23 to the product inspection personnel shall verify whether the corresponding product characteristics controlled by the tooling are qualified, and record them in ANF5032-23. After completing the record, ANF5032-23 shall be transmitted to the tooling inspection personnel for storage.

6.3.4 For tooling that fails verification, the technical personnel of the unit shall analyze it. If the tooling plan needs to be changed, the tooling shall be upgraded according to 14.7.2.1 "Tooling Application Management"; If it is a tooling manufacturing issue, submit a repair request to the process equipment room in accordance with 14.7.3.3 "Process Equipment Rework". After upgrading or repairing the tooling, continue to verify until it passes the verification.

After the tooling verification is qualified, the quality engineering department inspector of the tooling user unit shall stamp and indicate the date at the "verification" position on the tooling qualification certificate/certificate, and indicate the batch/shipment of products that have completed the tooling verification.
