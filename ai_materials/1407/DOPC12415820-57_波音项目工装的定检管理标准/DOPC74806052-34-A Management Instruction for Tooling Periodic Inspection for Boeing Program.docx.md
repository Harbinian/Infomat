Management Instruction for Tooling Periodic Inspection for Boeing Program

Scope

This management instruction specifies the requirements for tooling periodic inspection.

This management instruction is applicable to Boeing program production (except B787).

This management instruction is developed in accordance with requirements of DOPB14760685 and D33200-1.

Reference Documents

D33200-1          Boeing Supplier's Tooling

DOPB14760685      Management Procedure for Tooling Periodic Inspection

DOPB143760        Management Procedure for Development of Technical Document

DOPB080008        Management Procedure for Quality Records

3　 Form

None

3.2  Referenced Form

ANF3276-42     Frequency Determination Request for Periodic Tooling Inspection

Terms and Definitions

4.1  Periodic Tooling Inspection Plan (PTI Plan)/ Periodic Tooling Inspection Record

The documents prepared based on the function and design of tooling are the basis for periodic inspection of tooling. It includes the periodic inspection contents and theoretical data of tooling, and is also used to record the measurement data and inspection results of periodic inspection of tooling.

When it is used as the basis for periodic inspection of tooling, it is called periodic inspection plan of tooling. It is called periodic check record of tooling when it is used to record the data and inspection results of periodic inspection of tooling and attached with the report issued by measuring equipment.

Regulations

5.1  All the Category II tooling must be periodic inspected.

5.2 Category II tooling for product and inspect need to perform inspection for the first 3 consecutive ship set until the stability of the tooling is verified, then the tooling shall be periodic inspected per the periodic cycle time. According to the tooling experience, stability, function and design tooling periodic inspection cycle time initial determined and subsequent changes, must be approved by Boeing, using the ANF 6606-88 to ask for the customer to approve the periodic inspection cycle.

5.3  Periodic checking cycle time is no more than 6 months. The cycle time of PTI for Category II tooling is no more than 12 months.

5.4  For all class II tooling used for production and inspection, a tooling periodic inspection plan shall be prepared separately by technician in Tooling Office. The tooling periodic inspection plan shall be in Chinese and English, and the data unit is inch. The tooling periodic inspection plan shall be submitted to the customer for approval when the initial manufacturing and inspection of the tooling are completed, and the customer shall be informed of the time of the first tooling periodic inspection.

5.5  Tooling periodic inspection plan should be submitted to customers for approval when upgrading.

5.6  The elements included in tooling periodic inspection are A-O, and the elements included in tooling periodic check are A-H. See Appendix A for the layout and filling of tooling periodic inspection/check plan.

A. Revision of PTI plan.

B. Boeing tooling number.

C. Boeing lifetime serial number.

D. Tooling unit number.

E. Integrity of tooling parts.

F. Conformity between tooling object and latest Tooling Design Revision.

G. Tooling level and verticality requirements applicable to tooling periodic inspection / periodic check specified in tooling design.

H. Visual inspection for damage, wear and foreign object debris.

I. Temperature of tooling during PTI.

J. Tooling characteristics compatible with master tooling

K. Digital measurement of features such as optical tooling points, enhanced reference points, indexed, I/R features and any other critical dimensions per the tooling design.

L. “ Before” and “After” adjustment readings.

M. Area to log tooling non-conformances for out of tolerance conditions.

N. Before and after survey scale measurements (for CMS controlled tooling).

O. PTI tolerances: Tolerances used for PTIs must be in accordance with the tooling design requirement.

5.7  The technician in Tooling Office will upload the tooling periodic inspection plan to the CPM platform for management. The path is "Boeing general > Technological Innovation Department > Tooling Office > Self Prepared Technical Documents>Plan of Periodic Tooling Inspection and Check". During periodic tooling inspection, The operator will transmit the latest version of the periodic tooling inspection plan to the periodic tooling inspection. When accepting the tooling, the inspector of tooling user shall confirm that the tooling is periodic inspected according to the latest version of the periodic tooling inspection plan.

5.8  If an earthquake occurs within a radius of 50 miles, or natural disasters (such as hurricanes, floods, tornadoes, etc.) or man-made disasters may affect the use of tooling, The operator of periodic tooling inspection in tooling user contacts the Boeing tooling manager for guidance for relevant tooling that needs to be periodic inspected or periodic checked. 

6  Standard Legend Description

None

7  Record Control

7.1  Fill in and maintain relevant records according to DOPB080008.

7.2  The PTI’s record shall be stored ten years at least.
