Management Instruction for Tooling Acceptance for ORGB Program

1  Scope         This management instruction specifies requirements of tooling acceptance.

This management instruction is applicable to ORGB program production.

This management instruction is developed in accordance with the requirements SR0006 and DOPB74477081.1  Normative Reference Documents        The following documents are essential for the application of this document. For all referenced documents marked with a date or (and) version, only the version marked with a date or (and) version is applicable to this document. For any referenced document without a date or (and) version, the latest version (including all modifications) shall apply to this document.SR0006    Regulations of Tooling Design, Manufacturing, Acceptance and Management

DOPB84357235     Management Procedure for Tooling Acceptance

DOPB080008     Management Procedure for Quality Records

3  Form

None

4  Terms and DefinitionsNone

5  Regulations5.1 In the process of tooling acceptance, for assembly tooling that built on site(including assembly tooling used for inspection), tooling inspector of tooling user shall inspect correctness of tooling ball/surface by portable digital measurement device, put stamp with date on the cover of measurement report that issued by the device.

5.2 After the tooling project controlled by ORGB is qualified and accepted, the quality representative of ORGB shall check the tooling acceptance basis and the tooling, and the product can be produced only after the tooling is qualified.

5.3 For the controlled tooling project of ORGB, unless otherwise authorized, the newly made, installed, re-used or restructured type Ⅱ tooling and the type Ⅰ tooling with major repair shall be accepted by the representative of ORGB, and can be put into production and use after qualified.6  Standard Legend Description

None

7  Record Control

 

7.1  Relevant records should be filled and maintained in accordance with procedure DOPB080008 requirements.

7.2  The tooling quality records should be kept for at least 10 years for ORGB.
