# Data lineage review service

- Use the dedicated review volume. Expose the review service on host interfaces only as authorized by the user. Do not connect to business databases, production services, or employee identity sources.
- Keep the catalog read-only. Bind each review revision to the catalog digest and item identifier, preserve all submitted revisions, and distinguish evidence, open questions, and recorded decisions.
- Use Chinese labels on the desktop interface. Verify in Edge at 100% with a 1699 × 828 CSS-pixel content viewport.
- Keep extracted review materials and temporary verification results under ignored artifacts. Store runtime source and operating instructions here.
